# Make one moment loud at a time

The games Jacob points to make squad fights fun and readable with four habits. Only a few things are loud at once. Every threat starts from a visible source and has more than one answer. Synergy is picked and shown before the fight, then paid off one beat at a time. Each fight teaches one new thing. Kindred's fights have failed his plays on exactly these points. In his rules 12 fight about **7 notable events a second** hit the screen, with a peak of **28**, words sat on every body and the enemy ran the same combos as his squad; he said "i didnt know what was going on at all" ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11658](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11658)). Rules 13 cut the enemy back and staged one system per fight, and his play of it **still failed**: his leader died while all five others lived, his Holds had nothing to parry, and his combos showed as a generic COMBO HIT ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11726](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11726)). Lab research explains the overload, because people follow about four moving things and often miss a second event inside half a second of the first. The rules 13 play shows that cutting alone is not enough: the few things left must be loud, come from their source and match the rules. Most of the fix already sits in Jacob's own calls, from the free universal Hold to automatic kits, chains for his side only, trash that carries the chief's moves and fights that end when units fall. Codex is building rules 14 now (leader protection, parry cues from the attacker, real combo presentation), which matches three of the strongest findings, but nobody has played it. Everything else below is a proposal for Jacob, marked as one.

The table shows three shapes. **The pure auto battlers put every decision before the fight, keep fights short and force them to end.** Backpack Battles fights run 10 to 20 s with fatigue damage from 17 s, a TFT fight stops at 40 s, and Mechabellum rounds ran to 60 s in 2023 ([Backpack Battles Wiki, Fatigue](https://backpackbattles.wiki.gg/wiki/Fatigue); [LoL Wiki, TFT](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Teamfight_Tactics_%28game%29); [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2023/05/mechabellum-is-an-auto-battling-supreme-commander-im-completely-hooked/page=1/)). Warpack, Northgard Battlegrounds and Time Walker belong here too, as far as their own pages say, with the commander layer between fights. **The hands on games give a few verbs on different clocks and make each verb answer something visible.** That is Kingdom Rush, Clash Royale and Bad North. **The action games supply the threat language:** one answer that works on most things, a few loud exceptions and a rank that pays for variety. That is Sekiro, Devil May Cry, Hades and the raid bosses. Kindred sits between the first two shapes. Its units fight alone like TFT's, and its player gives orders like a Kingdom Rush player. Inference: it has to borrow the visible build from the first shape and the visible prompts from the second, or the orders have nothing to answer. Pure watching splits players. On one forum some never tire of watching their robots fight, while others say there is nothing to do once a battle starts ([Quarter To Three thread](https://forum.quartertothree.com/t/mechabellum-tactical-autobattler-against-other-people/167835)). Kindred's orders are its answer to that split, which is why they have to read.

## Eyes follow about four things, not twenty eight

Lab work sets hard limits on a watcher. People track about **four moving objects** at once, up to eight when they move slowly and only one when they move fast ([Alvarez and Franconeri 2007](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/faculty/research/detail/2007/how-many-objects-can-you-track-evidence-for-a?p=1)). They hold about **four objects** in visual working memory ([Luck and Vogel 1997](https://www.nature.com/articles/36846)). After noticing one event they often miss a second that lands within about **200 to 500 ms**, though one that comes straight after the first is often caught ([attentional blink review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2915904)). Small items beside busy shapes are hard to recognise away from where the eye points ([Pelli and Tillman 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2772078)), and a change goes unseen unless attention is already on it ([Rensink and others 1997](https://homepage.villanova.edu/diego.fernandezduque/Teaching/CognitivePsychology/Lectures_and_Labs/s3Attention/sReading/Rensink_etal_97.pdf)). Action game players track about two more items than other people ([Green and Bavelier 2006](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:153732)), but a new player starts at the lower number. Inference: a watcher takes in about **two to four notable events a second**, fewer if they must read words. At full speed, Kindred's rules 12 average of 7 is one every 143 ms, inside the blink that follows each event, and its peak of 28 is past any eye. Half speed, the default, halves both and still leaves about 14 in the peak second (inference from [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11719](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11719)). Slowing everything is no cure on its own either. Battlegrounds players already complain about waiting through long combat animations ([Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/battlegrounds-animation-issues/135955)).

Studios that ship busy fights rank their effects instead of turning them all up. Riot's rule for League is that "the amount of attention an ability grabs should match its level of importance", where importance means damage, crowd control, whether the player can dodge it and how much it changes play ([Riot, Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/clarity-in-league/)). World of Warcraft added a setting that keeps your own effects and enemy spells at full strength and turns other friendly effects down ([Icy Veins, Patch 11.2](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/too-many-spells-on-screen-patch-11-2-fixes-that-with-one-simple-setting/)). Overwatch answered its spectator problem partly by tinting every explosion and projectile in fixed team colours ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/3325/jeff-kaplan-addresses-the-spectator-issue-in-overwatch-responds-with-team-uniforms-top-down-map-and-more)). Zach Gage splits a screen into an instant read, a secondary read and a detail read, and names Slay the Spire's crowd of small icons as hard for viewers ([Gigazine report](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20180501-game-design-subway-legibility)). TFT gives its rare, decisive giant units the most screen ([Riot, Talking Tactics](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/talking-tactics-baron-versus-t-hex/)), and TFT and Backpack Battles both move the numbers into a recap after the fight ([GameWatcher](https://www.gamewatcher.com/teamfight-tactics-patch-12-6); [PC Games](https://www.pcgames.de/Indie-Games-Thema-239276/Specials/Backpack-Battles-Builds-Early-Access-Inventar-getestet-Auto-Battler-1442869/2/)). Bad North went furthest: few rules, hidden systems and sound clear enough to follow with your eyes shut ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/68638/indie-spotlight-plausible-concept-on-bad-north/); [Gaming Audio News](https://gamingaudionews.com/2018/04/26/bad-norths-developers-talk-about-inspiration-behind-audio-and-gameplay/)).

Feel follows the same rule. Sakurai freezes both fighters when a hit lands, longer for bigger hits up to a cap, and keeps the freeze shorter when more players share the screen because a third player can step in ([Source Gaming, Sakurai](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)). Smash Ultimate scales the freeze down to 0.75 at eight players ([SSBWiki, Hitlag](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Hitlag)). In a preregistered test of 1,699 players, feedback tied to the player's own success raised competence and curiosity, while louder feedback for its own sake lowered them ([Kao and others 2024](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/253c32f5-d124-4a23-88b5-9aaaf114608d)), and an earlier test found medium and high juice beat both none and extreme ([Pichlmair and Johansen 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.09201)). A Hearthstone analysis splits each big moment into a build up, a peak and an aftermath that stops the next action landing too soon ([JB Oger](https://jboger.substack.com/p/hearthstone)). A reviewer credits Kingdom Rush's humour with keeping a hard, hands on game from feeling overwhelming ([Pocket Gamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/036437/r/)), which fits his tone lock ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:81](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L81)). Inference: his "too subtle" and his "i didnt know what was going on" have one answer. **Make the big moments bigger by making the small ones smaller.**

What this means for Kindred, as a proposal and not his call: rank every event and give only the top one the full freeze, flash, big number and sound. A first order to test, built from Riot's factors, runs from anything he can answer right now (a parry beat, a cast that Focus Fire can break) to enemy telegraphs, deaths, his own combo payoffs, status changes and last, routine hits. Everything below the top plays small, with hit stop on the two fighters involved rather than the whole screen. Words come off the bodies, one fixed colour per side goes into every spark and projectile, and numbers wait for the recap. Some of this already exists. The 1 Oct build froze the fight for a big hit only if none had landed in the last 0.7 s, which is a rate limit ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:4530](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L4530)); whether that rule survives in today's code is not checked. Rules 13 swapped words on fighters for icons ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11719](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11719)), and the band's heavy swings already take turns, one at a time ([BattleSimulation.Heavy.cs:6](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.Heavy.cs#L6)). His bear call, a beast as big as the whole squad that hits the whole party, is TFT's giant unit rule in his own words ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:646](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L646)). A cap on how many notable enemy actions can be live at once, like the attack tokens DOOM (2016) used to limit how many demons attack together ([Game Developer, DOOM AI](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)), would be a sim rule, and the sim work now sits with Codex ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11649](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11649)).

## Every order should answer a threat on screen

Busy does not mean more events. It means a steady stream of readable choices. Kingdom Rush gives a cheap Reinforcements drop on a 10 to 15 s recharge that its wiki says is almost always worth using, hero ultimates mostly on 30 to 60 s, and one big area spell on 60 to 80 s ([KR Wiki, Reinforcements](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Call_Reinforcements); [KR Wiki, Alliance ultimates](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Spell/Alliance); [KR Wiki, Rain of Fire](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_of_Fire)). Ironhide's cofounder said they wanted the player involved and busy rather than planning and then watching ([Kodeco interview](https://www.kodeco.com/2769-making-a-hit-tower-defense-game-a-top-app-dev-interview)). Clash Royale drips one elixir every 2.8 s, which works out to about one card every 7 to 8 s (inference from [CR Wiki, Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir)), and its elixir trades score each card as an answer to the attack it stopped. In a study of over 3,000 StarCraft 2 players, even the lowest league finished a look and act cycle about every five seconds ([PLOS ONE, SkillCraft](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0075129)). There is a ceiling as well. Riot's fast Hyper Roll mode took away the calm turns, felt chaotic and more stressful than normal TFT, and was retired at about 2% of play ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/); [Riot, Gizmos and Gadgets learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-gizmos-gadgets-learnings/); [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/teamfight-tactics/tft-roadmap-2025-no-more-hyper-roll-pengus-party-returns-and-new-set-revival)). Valve's Left 4 Dead notes say combat that never changes tires players and long lulls bore them ([Booth 2009](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/apps/valve/2009/ai_systems_of_l4d_mike_booth.pdf)).

Jacob pressed **4 orders in 41 s** in his rules 12 fight ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11660](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11660)). Inference: that is close to Clash Royale's early rate of about 4 to 6 plays in the same time, so the count is not the main gap. **The gap is the prompt.** His calls already give Kindred Kingdom Rush's tiers: Hold is free and universal, Fall Back is a 1 point dodge, and points earned by good play pay for Focus Fire, Unleash and the chief spell ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11280](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11280); [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11529](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11529); [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:287](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L287)). What is missing is a visible reason for each press. Inference: aim for one readable beat every 5 to 10 s, never none, as in rules 13's first fight, and never 40 a minute, as in rules 8. A free button also needs a guard. On the road a Hold now costs nothing, lasts 5 s, cuts front row damage by 30% and drains no nerve ([BattleSimulation.cs:35](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.cs#L35); [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11440](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11440)). Inference: that is the kind of always safe button his 6 Oct chore note warns about. Sekiro's answer is to shrink the window when the player mashes, and the raid chief already keeps the old window if Hold is pressed again within 1 s ([BattleSimulation.Raid.cs:27](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.Raid.cs#L27)). Whether road fights have such a guard is not checked.

The action games show how a threat should look. Sekiro's deflect answers most attacks in a short window, about 0.2 s on a fan wiki, though summaries of the same page say 0.5 s and no official figure exists; pressing early gives a plain block, and mashing shrinks the window until a quiet gap or a success resets it ([Fextralife, Deflection](https://sekiroshadowsdietwice.wiki.fextralife.com/Deflection)). Its few exceptions share one alarm, a red symbol and a sound, and each needs a different answer read from the animation ([Activision blog](https://blog.activision.com/sekiro/2019-03/Sekiro-Shadows-Die-Twice-Strategies-Surviving-Your-First-Few-Hours-as-the-One-Armed-Wolf)). Players describe a good deflect as bright sparks and a crisp clang and a bad one as a dull clunk ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/814380/discussions/0/4209245188602176493/?ctp=1)), so the contrast is the feedback. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 runs the same tiers in a party game, a wide dodge that pays nothing and a tight parry that pays a point and a counter, and players complain where the parry became mandatory or its moment was unclear ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-parry-dodge-jump-in-clair-obscur-expedition-33/amp/); [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1903340/discussions/0/592895445665149150)). Slay the Spire added enemy intents because hidden moves did not fit permadeath, and playtesters found exact numbers more engaging ([Wikipedia, Slay the Spire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire)). Into the Breach shows every attack before it lands so that a death reads as the player's own fault ([Game Developer, Into the Breach](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-subset-games-i-into-the-breach-i-)). World of Warcraft's Midnight shows a timeline of upcoming boss abilities ([Blizzard](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24244638/how-midnights-upcoming-game-changes-will-impact-combat-addons)), and its encounter designers say they cut noise, moved to one standard set of indicators and let a failure play out over a few seconds so players see why (secondhand summary, [Seramate](https://seramate.com/news/2026/08/22/ellesmere-encounter-design-interview-1f19e39f)). A critic of Hades II says danger zones that light up away from the boss are hard to read ([Hades II legibility critique](https://nex-3.com/blog/hades-ii-has-a-legibility-problem/)).

Timing sets a floor under all of this. A simple reaction to one obvious cue takes about **265 ms**, and a move with no visible start cannot be answered at all ([Infil, On Reaction Times](https://ki.infil.net/reaction.html)). Picking between two cues took about 380 ms in one informal test ([Virtua Fighter forum](https://host.virtuafighter.com/threads/reaction-time-tests-on-different-games-and-setups.22576)). League's Baron winds up his big moves for 1.25 to 2 s, in a fixed cycle after a random first move ([LoL Wiki, Baron Nashor](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_Nashor)). Inference: with Kindred's half second Perfect Hold ([FightRules.cs:223](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/FightRules.cs#L223)), a tell needs to be readable about 1 to 1.5 s before the beat, and longer when the player must first find which of five fighters is the target. Kindred's band heavies and the carried Cleave tell for **1 s**, the Burning Ring for 1.5 s and the Brand for 4 s ([BattleSimulation.Heavy.cs:23](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.Heavy.cs#L23); [BattleSimulation.Carriers.cs:28](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.Carriers.cs#L28)). The 1 s tells sit at the short end, and on a screen with 7 events a second they vanish.

His locks already have Sekiro's shape. The parry negates anything ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:573](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L573)). Nothing is unparryable, a Hold given while paused never parries, and every kit's other answers stay as the safe choice ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:583](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L583) to [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:585](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L585)). Band swings are small beats every 5 s, and a Hold that is not a parry is only a stance ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:659](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L659); [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:660](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L660)). For the raid chief he asked that casts you can break show a cast bar while parry moves do not ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11170](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11170)), a two state flag like World of Warcraft's shielded and unshielded cast bars ([Wowhead guide](https://cn.classic.wowhead.com/tanking-guide)). The proposals follow from that, and none is approved. Show a short queue of the next two or three beats, read from the threat list the sim already hands the screen ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:10530](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L10530)), and when the queue is empty, let the Hold button show that nothing can be parried. Start every tell on the attacker's body and point it at the target. Make the perfect Hold and the early Hold look and sound clearly different. Show the 2 s stagger after a parry as a window over the staggered fighter, so the Riposte has an obvious moment ([FightRules.cs:226](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/FightRules.cs#L226); [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:582](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L582)). Let cast bars and healers prompt Focus Fire, and rings on the floor prompt Fall Back. Rules 14 already claims parry cues that start from the attacker ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11733](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11733)); not checked.

One more rule comes from the record. A rival's counter that lands on the same tick as the order it answers makes the order look like it did nothing, which matches the complaint Codex recorded, that learning immediately countered his successful play ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:10868](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L10868)). Riot kept a disable when players could see it coming and play around it, and cut a full counter to 80% so the countered build survived ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings)). ## Synergy reads when it is picked before the fight

In every game here where units fight on their own, the synergy is a fact the player can see before the fight. TFT counts traits against breakpoints such as 2, 4 and 6, so a player can splash a little or commit ([Invenglobal](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/11260/teamfight-tactics-galaxies-mid-set-update-includes-14-new-champions-3-unique-traits)), and Riot calls traits that only worked at six or nothing a failure ([Riot, Galaxies learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-galaxies-learnings/)). Backpack Battles lights a star on an item when the right neighbour sits beside it ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2427700/discussions/0/4290313152638370831)). The Bazaar prints adjacency on the card, such as an hourglass that speeds up the items next to it ([BazaarDB, Hourglass](https://www.bazaardb.gg/card/1sk99jmuf77zkyxy9rt7trq8u/Hourglass)). Warpack packs a backpack per unit, and Northgard Battlegrounds turns building placement into battle strength ([Steam, Warpack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5016130/); [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/northgard-battlegrounds-announced-for-pc)). Hades only offers a duo boon once both halves are held, and Hades II turns a met requirement green ([Fextralife, Funeral Pyre](https://hades2.wiki.fextralife.com/Funeral+Pyre); [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/hades-ii/p/SMarBe/Improved_Boon_Info_UI/)). Darkest Dungeon prints where a skill must stand and what it can hit as rows of dots, and Darkest Dungeon II shows its one combo token under each health bar ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/262060/discussions/0/412447613581265645); [DD wiki, Tokens](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens)). Time Walker's heroes fight with no input once the player has set their skills, gear and places ([Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20231130-273928/)).

**Chains that fire by themselves in the fight are where players get lost.** Super Auto Pets has five pets with one trigger word each, and players still could not follow the start of battle order; the developer agreed it was unclear ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1714040/discussions/0/3086646248547568129)). The Bazaar needed a hidden internal cooldown so items could not trigger each other in a loop ([Bazaar wiki, Items](https://thebazaar.wiki.gg/wiki/Items)). When a chain does read well, it is staged: a video essay on Balatro describes each card and joker stepping forward in turn while the score climbs with rising sound ([Balatro video transcript](https://amara.org/subtitles/qZaMrU6iDy4J/en/2/download/Balatros%20Cursed%20Design%20Problem.en.srt)). Working memory holds about four chunks ([Cowan 2001](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/magical-number-4-in-shortterm-memory-a-reconsideration-of-mental-storage-capacity/44023F1147D4A1D44BDC0AD226838496)), and Magic's head designer warns that the way cards interact on the table is the complexity that grows fastest ([Wizards of the Coast](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/new-world-order-2011-12-05)). Inference: a fight should ask a watcher to follow about four live synergies at most, each one chunk. Also by inference: in the PvE games here the enemy runs its own visible rules, not the player's engine. The games that mirror the player are async PvP, where the enemy really is another player's saved build.

His calls set the target. Bonds form from three combos by one pair, on any enemies. A bonded shot fires right away, because "right away is combo rest doesnt feel like a combo". Two stand in front and the back row are "constant combo doers". Every unit gets a kit of three used at proper intervals, and boosts fire on their own, which he liked because they are "what player has not to worry" ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11144](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11144) to [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11146](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11146); [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11158](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11158); [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11533](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11533)). His class rule bars giving one class another's spell ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:411](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L411)), so Time Walker's skills shared across classes do not transfer, though its pick first, watch it fire shape does. Inference: his "right away" instinct matches the lab, where a second event straight after the first is usually caught and one a few hundred milliseconds later is often missed. The record shows the gap. In a rules 12 bot sweep, setups left unclaimed outnumbered combos 909 to 633 ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11703](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11703)). In his rules 13 fight, fifteen real cashes, such as a pin and an Opening cashed on the same tick, all showed as a generic COMBO HIT with small glyphs ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11729](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11729)).

Proposals, none approved. Show the links before the fight: on the squad screen, draw a lit line between fighters who can chain, the way Backpack Battles lights a star, so he knows what to watch for. Show each payoff as one staged beat: the setter's mark lands on the target from the setter, the finisher strikes, and one stamp with both fighters' icons and the number lands while everything else stays small. Keep chains to his side, which rules 13 already does ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11717](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11717)), and keep early fights to a handful of link types. ## Teach one system per fight, starting with the parry

The teaching research agrees on one new thing at a time. Hayashida's Mario stages teach a mechanic, develop it, twist it, then test it ([Game Developer, Hayashida](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-secret-to-i-mario-i-level-design)). Valve added two simpler Portal chambers when playtests showed one puzzle brought in too many ideas at once, and noted that overwhelmed players stop taking in new information ([Portal commentary](https://combineoverwiki.net/wiki/Developer_commentary/Portal)). A community analysis of the Kingdom Rush campaign shows the second level adds one new enemy type, the fifth is a breather and the most unusual towers come last ([Game Developer, Harlow](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/kingdom-rush---the-wonderful-campaign-level-design)). In tests on over 45,000 players, tutorials raised play time by up to 29% only in the most complex game ([Andersen and others 2012](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/projects/game-abtesting/chi2012/)), and Celia Hodent notes that players can hardly read tutorial text while fighting ([Hodent slides](https://interaction19.ixda.org/download/talk-the-ux-of-fortnite-celia-hodent.pdf)). Monster Hunter's first monsters have simple attacks and easy tells ([Vice](https://vice.com/en/article/the-developers-of-monster-hunter-explain-what-its-like-to-build-monsters)). Sekiro lets players practise deflects on a partner who cannot hurt them ([Activision blog](https://blog.activision.com/sekiro/2019-03/Sekiro-Shadows-Die-Twice-Strategies-Surviving-Your-First-Few-Hours-as-the-One-Armed-Wolf)), and raid players praise trash that previews the boss's moves (opinion column, [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-01-26-taking-out-the-trash.html)).

Kindred's record follows the warning. Rules 12 put about twelve systems into one fight in a day ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11667](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11667)), the Portal mistake at full size. Jacob then approved one new system per road fight, combos first, then defence moves, then boosts, with carried moves last before the chief, and Codex built it as rules 13 ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11671](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11671); [HANDOFF_LOG.md:11717](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11717)). His call that road trash carries small versions of the chief's moves is the raid trash lesson in his own words ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11532](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11532)). **The miss was the parry.** Rules 13's first fight dropped heavy swings, so the move he calls the ultimate one had nothing to do in fight one ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11728](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11728)). Rules 14 puts ordinary heavy parry beats back into stage one, as claimed and not checked ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11733](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11733)). Inference: the order that fits the research is the parry first, alone, against one beat at a time, then one new system per fight. An enemy gains a system only after he has seen it on his own side, a breather follows each new one, and any words wait for the screen between fights.

The fight must also keep its promises. Bad North makes a squad's commander unkillable while any of his soldiers stands ([Bad North Wiki, Commander](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Commander)). Players hand work to automation only when it is reliable ([Game Developer, Rules of Engagement](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/rules-of-engagement)), and feedback that suggests something other than what the rules do makes a game frustrating and hard to learn ([Pichlmair and Johansen 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.09201)). Jacob listed self preserving play among his priorities ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11155](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11155)), and after rules 13 he authorised leader protection, which Codex is building as a leader who is safe while any ally stands ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11733](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11733)). ## Conclusion

The research moves Kindred's problem from systems to beats. The game has rarely lacked mechanics. It lacks a few readable beats, each with a source, an answer and a payoff. Every pass that added systems pushed the beat rate past what eyes can follow, and the one pass that cut hard removed the beat the parry needed. So there is one number to watch, the loud events a second and whether each has a cause he can see, and one rule that answers both of his complaints at once: make the big moments bigger by making the small ones smaller. His own instincts already point this way. "Right away is combo" is how perception joins two events into one, his one second look rule is Gage's instant read, and his call to "sync nerve bar and all units together" is the alive or dead read that every spectator screen puts first.

The judge is his next play, not a bot count, under his build first rule ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:301](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L301)). A good check, Codex's and acknowledged by Claude but not locked, is whether after a fight he can say why one enemy fell and why one ally survived without opening Details ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:11232](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L11232)). It matters past feel, because people who have never seen the game play it at the 30 Nov player test ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:309](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L309); [HANDOFF_LOG.md:10586](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L10586)). How sure: **LIKELY** on the diagnosis, because the lab limits, his six plays and the sixteen games all point the same way. **GUESS** on every number here (two to four events a second, a beat every 5 to 10 s, tells 1 to 1.5 s early), since each comes from analogy and needs his play to set it. Rules 14 is not checked.

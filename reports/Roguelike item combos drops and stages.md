# Make one pick pay off everywhere

Kindred Siege gets real builds when each item switches on something that other fighters, road stages and the hunters all read, so one pick pays off in several places. What that shared thing should be is Jacob's choice, and this report ends with three options: statuses, trophy parts or tribes. Bad news first: none of it exists yet. Every item today raises one stat, only four chief pieces add a special, a won fight offers one fixed piece and never a choice of pieces, and the road has only fights and three placeholder events, with no beast, elite or envoy in the code. Other games prove each part. Darkest Dungeon's Mark lets one hero set up another hero's big hit, and Teamfight Tactics' Emblems let one item change a whole team's bonus. Slay the Spire, Hades and Risk of Rain 2 pair a small pick after most fights with a guaranteed prize at bosses. FTL, Darkest Dungeon and Battle Brothers make players value a crew member or an item because events and beasts check for it. Kindred's locks fit this better than they look. One gear slot per fighter gives a squad five slots, like Balatro's five Jokers, so combos have to live between fighters. The rule that no chance is ever shown pushes stage checks toward fixed thresholds written in words. The biggest risk for a solo developer is uneven coverage, where some picks never get a hook. Kindred facts below cite the code or the locks. Everything else said about Kindred is inference, and none of it has been played.

## Five gear slots push every combo between fighters

### Proven in other games: three shapes make two picks worth more together

The games researched make two picks worth more together in three repeat shapes. In squad games the two picks sit on different units, and an item is often the switch. The first shape is applier and exploiter. In Darkest Dungeon, Mark has no effect by itself. It switches on bonus damage from other heroes, and three classes roughly **double their damage against a marked target** ([DD wiki, Mark](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Mark)). It cuts both ways, because enemies pick a marked hero **about 67 to 87% of the time**, depending on difficulty (same page). A trinket can push a hero into the exploiter role: the Shieldbreaker's Fanged Spear Tip adds 35% damage against marked targets but cuts the wearer's damage by 10% ([DD wiki, Trinkets](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets)). Darkest Dungeon II runs the same idea through a Combo token, **at most one per unit**, shown under every health bar so the player can see the link ([DD wiki, Tokens](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens)).

The second shape is the recipe. Hades has **28 duo boons**, each unlocked by holding one qualifying boon from each of two gods ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/hades-duo-boons-list-how-to-get/)). Vampire Survivors evolves a maxed weapon once you hold its partner passive and open a chest, and one passive, Hollow Heart, feeds three different weapons, so the passive pool stays small as weapons grow ([VS wiki, Evolution](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution)). Teamfight Tactics lets any two components make a finished item ([LoL wiki, TFT:Item](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Item)). Its lead designer said that rule made items easy to learn but produced very strong synergies, and the team chose to lean into extreme outcomes instead of filing them down ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/teamfight-tactics-design)).

The third shape counts tags across the team. Teamfight Tactics builds around traits shared between units ([Riot dev blog](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-design-pillars-of-tft/)), and a Spatula plus a component makes an Emblem that hands a unit a trait, so one item can change the whole team's bonus (LoL wiki page above). Monster Hunter World armour switches on a set bonus at two or more pieces of one set ([Fextralife, Skills](https://monsterhunterworld.wiki.fextralife.com/Skills)).

The games with the clearest builds also cap slots hard. Super Auto Pets fields **five pets**, each holding one food perk, and start of battle effects fire in a fixed order, highest attack first ([SAP wiki, The Basics](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Basics); [SAP wiki, Food](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Food); [SAP wiki, Glossary](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Glossary)). Balatro gives most runs **five Joker slots**, and order matters: Blueprint copies the Joker to its right ([Balatro wiki, Jokers](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers)). Risk of Rain 2 shows how to stop chains running away. Bleed and Burn **never trigger other items**, and chance stats stack on a curve, so one Tougher Times blocks about 13% of hits and ten block only 60% ([RoR2 wiki, Proc Coefficient](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Proc_Coefficient); [RoR2 wiki, Item Stacking](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Item_Stacking)).

### Inferred for Kindred: each shape already has a half built hook

Kindred locks **one gear slot per fighter** ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:283](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L283)). With five fighters that is the Super Auto Pets and Balatro shape: five slots, one item each. A combo cannot pile up inside one fighter, so it has to live between fighters, which is the squad combo the question asks for.

Today nothing can combine. Each piece raises one stat by 5, 10 or 15 ([CampaignGear.cs:80](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignGear.cs#L80)). Only the four chief Princess pieces carry a special, and spoils and event pieces never do ([Campaign.cs:2293](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L2293)). The three combos that exist, Ambush, Pyre and Riposte, chain orders only. Pyre can only be finished by an Eclipse, so it cannot fire until the Bloodridge chief is captured ([BattleSimulation.cs:975](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.cs#L975)). One special also promises more than it does. The Skullpeak piece says big hits on the wearer are halved, but the code only halves hits tagged as an Eclipse, so Dust Shark, Tide, Blood Call and heavy swings land in full ([BattleSimulation.cs:1515](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Battle/BattleSimulation.cs#L1515)). That is read from the code, not checked in a fight.

Still, each shape has a half built hook. For statuses, Stoneveil's Bleeding special wears targets down, while the Thundercoast Predator special and the Clarth Hunters trait both hit anyone below half health 25% harder, and those two bonuses add up ([CampaignGear.cs:93](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignGear.cs#L93); [TribeTraits.cs:138](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/TribeTraits.cs#L138)). That is an applier and an exploiter nobody planned. For recipes, the Pal who breaks a chief learns his gear recipe ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:88](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L88)), and the Clarth's signature pieces are already made from sand shark parts: jaw shield, spine rifle, fin blades, tooth net and tooth totem staff ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:101](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L101)). For tag counting, two or three fighters of one tribe already unlock its set and doctrine ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:285](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L285)), so an Emblem style piece that lets one fighter count toward another tribe would turn gear into a set builder. Rows are a free extra axis. Each fighter already stands front or back ([Campaign.cs:1014](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L1014)), so a piece that helps whoever shares its row makes formation part of the build.

What keeps this clear of the ideas Jacob rejected is that the item sets the role, not the class. A Warden can wear the piece that marks and a Skirmisher the piece that cashes in, or the other way round. No class is just doing its own job, no order belongs to a class, and nobody gets a new button. Two guards stop items sliding back into per fighter gambits. Items should change what a hit does, never whom a fighter targets. Triggers should fire in a fixed integer order, like Super Auto Pets' highest attack first, so replays and checksums hold. Every status needs one icon and one colour under the health bar, as in Darkest Dungeon II, because fights are fast and every look must read in a second ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:82](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L82)). Pal favours can join cheaply too. Four Pals make only **six pairs**, so a full set of Hades style duo favours is six pieces. Class pairs grow from **10 with the starting five to 136 with all 17 classes** (simple arithmetic), so hand made pair content only scales while the set stays that small.

## Strip one of three, and guarantee the big prizes

### Proven in other games: short runs guarantee their build moments

Rewards in these games come in three layers. After most fights there is a small pick, usually one of three. Hades offers three boons from a god ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-olympian-god-boons-skills/2)), and Vampire Survivors three or four per level ([VS wiki, Level up](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Level_up)). At fixed beats there is a bigger prize from its own pool. Slay the Spire boss relics are a pick of one from three and come only from bosses ([Slay the Spire wiki, Relics](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Relics)). Risk of Rain 2 boss items never come from chests ([Rogue Ranker](https://rogueranker.com/yellow-items-ror2/)). Darkest Dungeon boss expeditions always pay a Very Rare trinket ([DD wiki, Trinkets list](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets_%28Darkest_Dungeon%29)). Then there is a spend point. Balatro's shop reroll costs $5 and rises $1 each time ([Balatro wiki, Shop](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Shop)), and players report that a Risk of Rain 2 printer swaps a random item of yours for the one it shows ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/632360/discussions/0/1768134097435781647)).

The steering found in these games runs on rules the player can see. Hades only offers a duo once you hold both halves ([Hades Fextralife wiki, Boons](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Boons)). Slay the Spire's Bottled relics only appear if the deck holds a matching card type, and each relic shows up once per run (Relics page above). Even the size of the pick is a dial: Busted Crown gives an extra energy but shows two fewer card choices ([Slay the Spire wiki, Relics List](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Relics_List)). Some games show an item on a unit before you can take it. Wildfrost's Bells put charms on enemies ([Wildfrost wiki, Charms](https://wildfrostwiki.com/Charms)), and Teamfight Tactics puts an item on every carousel unit to make the pick more interesting (Riot dev blog above).

Odds and guarantees split by run length. A Hades run can include **up to 72 chambers** ([Hades wiki](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Chambers_and_Encounters)). Games built on long loot grinds accept tiny odds, like Dead Cells' **0.4%** rare blueprints ([Dead Cells wiki, Blueprint](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Blueprint)). Slay the Spire guarantees its big moment instead: boss card rewards are always rare ([Slay the Spire Fandom, Card Rewards](https://slay-the-spire.fandom.com/wiki/Card_Rewards)). Where odds exist, bad luck protection is a counter. Slay the Spire's rare chance starts at minus 5%, rises 1% per common, stops at plus 40% and resets on a rare ([Slay the Spire wiki, Cards](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Cards)). The strongest pieces carry a cost in plain words. Ectoplasm gives energy but stops all gold, and Coffee Dripper gives energy but stops resting (Relics List above). Darkest Dungeon's Accuracy Stone adds 4 accuracy and takes 1 speed (Trinkets list above). Pools also grow across runs, and that has a known cost. A design blog on Isaac argues every weak unlock dilutes the chance of a good item ([On Games And Design](https://urflow.bearblog.dev/roguelites-the-binding-of-isaac)), and banning unlocked weapons from the loot table was one of the features Dead Cells players asked for most ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/dead-cells/custom-game-mode-patch)).

### Inferred for Kindred: the strip lock already allows a choice

Kindred runs are short, so each pick weighs more. Against Hades' 72 chambers, a Kindred run is two roads, two chiefs and the hunt, about **6 to 8 fights** by our count of the road code. Yet a won fight offers one fixed piece, its stat picked by the fight seed, unless it is the row's favour node ([Campaign.cs:1517](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L1517)). Banking materials is a good skip already. The choice is what is missing.

The lock says a won fight lets you strip one piece of their gear ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:277](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L277)). It does not say which piece. So the beaten squad could wear real gear in the fight, and after a win you strip one of three pieces they wore, or bank. You meet the gear before you own it, like Wildfrost's charmed enemies, so the fight teaches what the piece does. The no duplicates rule stays ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:336](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L336)). Steer with visible gates, like the Bottled relics: offer a piece that cashes in a status only if someone already applies it, or a recipe's second half only if the first is held. Tie each piece to a tribe and dilution stays in check, because a run meets only two road tribes ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:405](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L405)), so each run's pool stays small as tribes are added.

Quality is already earned, not rolled. Tiers come from the node type, objectives and omens ([CampaignGear.cs:107](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignGear.cs#L107)). That suits a short run and Jacob's rule that no chance is shown anywhere ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:291](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L291)). If a roll is ever added, the Slay the Spire counter is one integer that fits the save snapshot. Costs on strong pieces should read as words, like "cannot Fall Back", never as a percent.

Three gaps need Jacob's call. A player who always captures never sees a special, because kill gives the Princess piece and capture gives the tribe ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:275](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L275)). The lock says elites drop Princess too, but no elite encounter exists ([CampaignRules.cs:26](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignRules.cs#L26)). And the spend side is thin. The only shop is the Bone Trader event ([CampaignRoad.cs:116](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignRoad.cs#L116)), and the only recipe is the Lava Sun Tabard, forged once at camp for 50 materials and worn every run after ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:233](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L233)). A materials reroll that costs more each time would be a real price, because materials are the one thing that goes home ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:188](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L188)).

## Beasts, chores and envoys earn their place by reading the squad

### Proven in other games: stages that test what you carry make picks matter

The games researched use two layers. A map shows what each node is and pays before you commit, like the god symbols on Hades' doors (Hades Boons page above). Then events give most options to anyone and add one that only the right squad sees. FTL's blue options appear only with a certain crew race, system, weapon, drone or augment ([FTL Wiki, Blue Options](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Options)). A 2012 review called them always far better than a normal choice ([Alternative Magazine Online](https://alternativemagazineonline.co.uk/2012/11/19/game-review-ftl-faster-than-light-pc/)), and modders found that an event with only blue options never shows ([Subset forum](https://subsetgames.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=44321)). In Darkest Dungeon, Holy Water on a Holy Fountain always removes 20 stress and heals, while a bare hand gets a coin flip ([DD wiki, Curio](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Curio)). Darkest Dungeon II lets carried parts decide whether a fight happens at all: cross a Hazardous or Rough road without a spare Armor or Wheel and you are ambushed ([DD wiki, Node](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Node)).

Beast fights in these games make the body the battlefield. Players report that Monster Hunter rolls separate loot for each broken part, and separate rewards for capture and kill ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/582010/discussions/0/1735462352481591861)). Battle Brothers' Lindwurm fills two tiles, and melee hits on it risk acid that eats armour, so polearms and ranged weapons become the safe pick. Its scales then make a cloak that blocks that acid ([GameBanshee, Dev Blog #98](https://www.gamebanshee.com/k4prf); [GameBanshee, Dev Blog #103](https://www.gamebanshee.com/k444u)). Fire Emblem: Three Houses gives each tile of a monster its own barrier, and breaking them all leaves it unable to move for a while ([Fire Emblem Wiki, Barrier](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Enclosing_Wall); [Perfectly Nintendo](https://www.perfectly-nintendo.com/?p=434331)). The warning is XCOM 2's Alien Rulers, which acted after every soldier action ([XCOM.com](https://xcom.com/news/en-into-the-nest-xcom-2-s-archon-king-exposed/amp)). Players called it cheating, and the expansion limited reactions to moves and attacks the Ruler could see ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/268500/discussions/0/2592234299564602359); [Ludo guide](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/xcom-2/dlc-content-overview/alien-rulers-alien-hunters-dlc)).

In these games, chores show their result later. Slay the Spire's three keys cost a rest, a relic and a fight with a burning elite, and together they open a fourth act ([Slay the Spire wiki, Keys](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Keys)). XCOM 2 covert actions take soldiers away on a timed job with an ambush risk, and three against one Chosen reveal its stronghold ([XCOM.com](https://xcom.com/news/xcom-2-war-of-the-chosen-covert-actions/amp); [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/xcom-2-war-chosen-how-covert-actions-what/)). Battle Brothers contracts change the world. Clearing direwolves brings villagers back, and a failed contract can leave granaries burned ([GameBanshee, Dev Blog #95](https://www.gamebanshee.com/kwpkr)).

Where talk replaces a fight, it pays a different coin and has a cost. Griftlands gives every character a separate negotiation deck, and killing a beaten foe pays cards but turns their friends against you ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/griftlands-complex-double-deck-building-shows-promise-in-early-access); [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/griftlands/griftlands-review-great-friends-in-low-places)). PC Gamer's review found that building for talk punished you in the fights you cannot avoid ([PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/griftlands-review)). Sparing in Undertale gives no EXP, so mercy costs power ([Wikipedia, Undertale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undertale)). Josh Sawyer swapped percentage checks for fixed skill thresholds in Fallout: New Vegas because random checks made players reload ([GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/k9kac)).

### Inferred for Kindred: four stage types, each reading the shared list

The road already has the map layer: every node shows what it is and what it drops ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:322](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L322)). But it has only two node kinds, fight and event ([CampaignRoad.cs:8](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignRoad.cs#L8)). The missing layer is stages that read the squad. If they read the same list the gear writes, whether that list holds statuses, trophy parts or tribes, every stage both tests a build and feeds one.

| Stage | How it reads the squad | What it pays | Lock it must respect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region beast | Its rows are body parts, and each part shrugs off one status, row or tribe, so other picks shine | Each broken part drops its own material for the Pals to forge | No riding monsters ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:100](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L100)) |
| Chore | Asks for something you carry, or sends one fighter away for a row | Skips or changes a later node in plain sight, like fewer blades in the ambush lane or a chief who starts with less nerve | The roster is your lives ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:279](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L279)) |
| Envoy | Terms depend on a status, trophy or tribe you hold, with thresholds written in words | The war party stands aside, pays tribute, or names the habit its chief learned | Nobody joins in path fights ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:274](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L274)) |
| Event | One or two plain choices, plus one that only shows for something you hold | The best option goes to the build that earned it | An event costs one fight on that road ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:323](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L323)) |

Two details are Kindred's own. First, a beast that breaks by parts, not nerve, makes the nerve half of Unleash do nothing for that fight. That is one plain way to make Unleash spam lose somewhere, which is still an open problem ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:256](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L256)). Second, envoys carry a price Undertale would recognise. Tribes learn from measured fight records ([LessonRules.cs:8](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/LessonRules.cs#L8)), so a fight you talk past gives them nothing to learn from, but you strip nothing and bank less. Following Griftlands' lesson, an envoy should read the squad you built for fighting, not ask for a separate talk build or a talking class, because chief fights are always forced. Every gated choice also needs a plain choice beside it, which is FTL's rule, and the best events pair a judgement call with the build reward.

Beasts are the biggest unknown and the biggest cost. Each tribe has a monster it hunts ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:100](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L100)), but only the Clarth's sand shark is named, and in code it is only a spell. There is no monster unit, encounter, drop or codex entry, and the 8 Oct question on where monsters belong, under chiefs, as side jobs or roaming, has no recorded answer ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:5202](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L5202)). Part drops fit the lock that Pals forge gear from what you won ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:88](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L88)), and driving a beast off versus killing it can echo capture versus kill. For a solo developer the cheap version is one large sprite with a few breakable zones, not five animated parts. Its big moves should reuse the heavy swing windup that Hold can already parry, not XCOM style hidden extra turns.

## Favourites grow where one pick has hooks everywhere

### Proven in other games: even coverage and a visible trail

Several games make content check for specific picks. Battle Brothers' event system launched with 47 events, some needing brothers with certain backgrounds or traits ([ModDB, Dev Blog #43](https://www.moddb.com/news/dev-blog-43-introducing-the-event-system)). It later added traits that brothers gain or lose from what happens to them, such as a fear of greenskins after a bad defeat ([GameBanshee, Dev Blog #111](https://www.gamebanshee.com/k4hc8)). Darkest Dungeon II builds affinity between heroes who keep hitting the same enemy, or one an ally debuffed ([DD wiki, Relationships](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Relationships)). The known failure is uneven coverage. One FTL player count found **10 crew events for Slugs but only 2 each for Zoltan and Mantis** ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/212680/discussions/0/4637114675688929745)). Obsidian had people check that every skill in Fallout: New Vegas got enough good content ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/fallout/fallout-new-vegas-director-josh-sawyer-says-obsidian-has-karma-police-who-go-through-the-game-making-sure-every-skill-gets-enough-cool-stuff-to-do-were-not-gonna-leave-you-out-to-dry/)). Backpack Battles' early access got 6 out of 10 from IGN, partly for having too few items to make runs differ ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles)).

Discovery runs on a visible trail. Vampire Survivors added info on found evolutions to its level up panels (VS Evolution page above). Balatro's collection marks unfound Jokers as Not Discovered and shows locked ones with their unlock condition (Jokers page above). Risk of Rain 2 fills a logbook entry the first time you pick an item up ([RoR2 wiki, Items](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Items)). Counters in these games tax a build rather than ban it. Balatro's The Ox sets your money to zero if you play your most played hand during that boss round ([Balatro wiki, Boss Blinds](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Boss_Blinds)). Slay the Spire's designer said they wanted "not just a single dominant strategy" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/road-to-the-igf-mega-crit-games-i-slay-the-spire-i-)).

### Inferred for Kindred: run traits, a rival that taxes, a codex that hints

A pick becomes a favourite when it has hooks in several places at once. If the same thing is granted by gear, read by envoys and events, feared by a beast part and learned by the hunters, players will love it or route around it, and either way they notice it. Traits can come from the road too. Kindred already has one trait that reacts to the run: Grudge hits a tribe that captured one of yours harder ([CampaignGear.cs:204](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignGear.cs#L204)). Run traits earned in stages, like the fighter who broke the beast's jaw or came home alone from a chore, belong to that fighter whatever the class, so they avoid the rejected traits that only make a class do its job. A Darkest Dungeon II style bond between two fighters who keep hitting the Focus Fire target suits the Kindred name. It should only come in good versions, since a random bad bond would be hard to read in a fast fight.

The hunters are the built in brake. The lock already says they counter your sets and favourite tricks inside a run ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:297](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L297)), but the code only learns against the order you gave most ([HunterCompany.cs:129](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/HunterCompany.cs#L129)). A hunter that learns to shrug off your most used status, trophy or tribe, shown before the fight the way a fight card already names a tribe's newest lesson ([RematchClue.cs:5](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/RematchClue.cs#L5)), would tax a favourite like The Ox without banning it.

Discovery needs the codex to change shape. Today it is a flat list of text lines, and it never records gear pieces, combos or monsters ([Campaign.cs:1654](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L1654)). A combo page with blanks and hints, filled the first time a combo fires, would teach at Kindred's speed. So would the fight history replay pointing at the link that paid off, since finished fights are already archived for replays ([Campaign.cs:575](../Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L575)). Last comes a coverage budget. List every status, part or tribe in play, count its hooks across gear, events, beasts and envoys, and fix the thin ones before adding more.

## Conclusion

The research moves the question. Kindred does not need stronger items. It needs one short list that gear writes and every stage, envoy and hunter reads, and the real decision is what goes on that list. Whatever goes on it, the cost for a solo developer is coverage, not code. Adding an entry is cheap. Giving every entry a hook in each kind of stage is not. So a first slice with a few entries, each hooked everywhere, beats a long list with thin hooks, and a plain count of hooks per entry is the check before anything new goes in. Confidence: LIKELY that these patterns carry over, since several games show each one. GUESS on how any of it feels in Kindred, which only Jacob playing it can settle under the build first rule ([DESIGN_LOCKS.md:301](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L301)).

Three directions, each a different answer to what goes on that list:

| Direction | What makes it different |
|---|---|
| Status web | Gear lets any fighter apply or cash in a few shared statuses, so builds grow from many small links between fighters inside each fight. |
| Trophy recipes | Beasts and chiefs drop named parts that the Pals fuse into big named pieces, so builds come from choosing which part to break and which recipe to chase. |
| Tribe stacking | Gear counts toward tribe sets like Teamfight Tactics Emblems, and envoys and chores trade in set pieces, so builds come from which tribes you collect each run. |

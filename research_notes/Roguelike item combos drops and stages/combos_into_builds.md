# Item combos that become builds: squad games and single character roguelikes, mapped to Kindred Siege

Researched 19 Oct 2026. Fandom wikis (Hades, Monster Train, Battle Brothers) refused direct fetches (HTTP 402), so those games lean on gaming press, store pages, Wikipedia and forums. Tags used below: **[fan]** means a forum post, player guide or fan analysis. **(via search summary)** means the fact came from a search engine summary of that page, not from a full read of it. Everything under "Inferences" is my reasoning, not a sourced fact.

Kindred Siege facts used for the fit notes come from `DESIGN_LOCKS.md` (read 19 Oct 2026): auto battle plus refilling order buttons; Pals forge gear from what you won and the Pal who broke a chief learns that chief's gear recipe; gear changes a unit's look, no mutations; losing a run loses run gear but materials, codex and chief memories stay; each chief gives his one main order for the rest of the run; the 10 Oct direction says all four Pals stay at camp as upgraders; "making one button spam lose" is still open.

## 1. How do item systems create combos between two or more characters in a squad, not just within one character?

### Takeaway
Squad games make cross character combos with five repeatable shapes: an applier plus an exploiter of one status (Darkest Dungeon Mark, DD2 Combo token), tags that count across the squad and items that add a tag (TFT traits and Emblems), position or adjacency effects (TFT same row items, DD1 position trinkets, Fire Emblem supports, Battle Brothers shieldwall, Super Auto Pets order), pair bonds that grow from fighting together (Fire Emblem supports, DD2 relationships), and squad wide zone items (Monster Train 2 rooms). Items are usually the switch that turns a unit into an applier, an exploiter, or a member of a tag.

### Cited Findings

**Teamfight Tactics (TFT)**
- Item types: a Component gives a base stat; a Completed Item is "made by combining 2 Components"; Spatula or Frying Pan plus a Component makes an Emblem that gives a trait; two Spatulas or Frying Pans make a Tactician's item that increases team size by 1; Support Items buff your team and/or debuff the enemy; Radiant and Artifact items are rarer power tiers. A champion holds up to 3 items. The page lists Set 18 first. ([LoL wiki, TFT:Item](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Item))
- Crafting works "via the item bench recipe list", by dragging one component onto another, or onto a champion that holds a component. Combining two copies of a champion who each hold a component crafts the completed item. An Emblem crafted on a champion that already has that trait drops off automatically. Temporary mid combat components cannot be crafted together. ([LoL wiki, TFT:Item](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Item))
- Cross unit item example, Zeke's Herald (support item, only from a Support anvil or an Augment, present Sets 10 to 14, now marked removed): "Grant 30% Attack Speed to the holder and allies within 2 hexes in the same row." Patch history shows the range changed over time. ([LoL wiki, Zeke's Herald](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Zeke%27s_Herald))
- Riot's pillars are "Mastery, playful competition, and discovery". Knowledge skill is described as "assembling complex and interlocking trait synergies". In shared draft (the carousel), "we put an item on each champion to make the decision even more interesting." (Volty, 18 Jun 2019) ([Riot dev blog](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-design-pillars-of-tft/))
- GDC 2020, lead designer David Abecassis (reported by Pocket Tactics): TFT simplified Auto Chess items so any two components combine; that made items accessible but created some very powerful synergies; the stated lesson was to "always embrace extreme outcomes". The game is about "tough choices, not touch execution". ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/teamfight-tactics-design))

**Darkest Dungeon 1**
- Mark "does nothing on its own"; it switches on bonus damage from other skills. Appliers: Arbalest Sniper's Mark, Bounty Hunter Marked for Death and Come Hither, Houndmaster Target Whistle, Musketeer Call the Shot, Occultist Vulnerability Hex (most also debuff DODGE or PROT). Arbalest, Bounty Hunter and Houndmaster roughly double their damage against marked targets. Marks usually last 3 turns and cannot be resisted, but the applying skill can miss. The wiki says mark appliers should be much faster than the rest of the party. ([DD wiki, Mark](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Mark))
- Mark cuts both ways: enemies are far more likely to target a marked hero (about 67 to 87% depending on difficulty), several enemies mark heroes, and some (Carrion Eater, Spitter, Swine Prince, Webber) deal +100% to marked heroes. Arbalest's Rallying Flare clears marks from teammates. ([DD wiki, Mark](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Mark))
- Trinkets: two slots per hero; each class has its own trinkets; many trinkets carry penalties. Examples: Fanged Spear Tip (Shieldbreaker) +35% DMG vs Marked, minus 10% DMG; Bloody Dice (Jester) +30% Bleed skill chance, minus 10% Bleed resist; Sniper's Ring (Arbalest) +15 ACC and +4% CRIT when in position 4, minus 2 SPD; Dazzling Charm +10% Stun skill chance. Crimson Court DLC trinkets come as two piece sets per hero with an extra bonus when both are worn, for example Crime Lords' Molars (Bounty Hunter) +20% DMG vs Marked, Stunned and Bleeding targets, set bonus +5% CRIT vs those targets. ([DD wiki, Trinkets](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets))
- Bleed and Blight tick at the start of the victim's turn, usually for 3 turns, ignore PROT, and a crit adds 2 turns. Unholy skeletons have very high bleed resistance and blight works better on bone or stone enemies. Bleed specialists include Flagellant, Highwayman, Houndmaster, Bounty Hunter, Hellion, Jester; blight specialists include Plague Doctor, Grave Robber, Antiquarian, Abomination, Shieldbreaker. Some skills lower the enemy's resist first (Highwayman Open Vein for bleed, Grave Robber Poison Darts for blight). The wiki flags its resistance tables as flawed. ([DD wiki, Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon)))

**Darkest Dungeon 2**
- Combo is a token "Consumed by various skills to trigger additional effects"; lasts 3 turns; limit 1 per unit. Other team tokens: Guard redirects the next attack and a unit can guard or be guarded by only one unit at a time; Riposte fires only from the frontmost unit holding it; Vulnerable adds 50% to the next damaging hit. Tokens are shown below each combatant's health bar. ([DD wiki, Tokens](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens))
- Applier and consumer pairs (via search summary, guide level detail): Highwayman's Point Blank Shot applies Combo and Grapeshot Blast hits two enemies when the target has Combo ([TechRaptor Highwayman guide](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/darkest-dungeon-2-highwayman-guide-skills-and-shrines)); Bounty Hunter has a 50% chance to reapply Combo when he consumes it ([DD wiki, Bounty Hunter DD2](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Bounty_Hunter_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))); Grave Robber plus Jester is a recommended pair because one applies Combo and the other gets bonus crit against comboed targets ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/darkest-dungeon-2/darkest-dungeon-2-beginner-tips/)) [fan].
- Relationships: each hero pair has affinity 0 to 20, starting at 9. Combat gains include attacking an enemy "debuffed or comboed by an ally" consecutively, attacking the same enemy consecutively, and healing, buffing or guarding a struggling ally. When leaving the Inn each pair rolls: affinity 17 to 19 gives 65% chance of a positive bond, 1 to 4 gives 65% negative. Positive bonds: Respectful (partner gets Strength, chance to follow up attacks), Hopeful (stress relief), Amorous (heals 10% max HP, can intercept attacks). Negative: Suspicious (Taunt and Vulnerable on partner), Envious, Hateful (may hit the partner), Resentful (Blind and Weak). ([DD wiki, Relationships](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Relationships))

**Monster Train and Monster Train 2**
- Store page: "With five clans to choose from"; "Pick your primary and supporting clan to gain access to all cards from both"; "Upgrade your clan specific champion multiple times"; "Upgrade any card twice"; "25 covenant (difficulty) levels"; "Each clan has 10 levels to unlock, bringing new cards to your deck"; build from "10 different starterdecks". ([Steam, Monster Train](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1102190/Monster_Train/))
- The allied clan adds cards but no champion ([Checkpoint Gaming](https://checkpointgaming.net/features/2020/03/monster-train-beta-impressions-save-the-pyre/), via search summary). Each clan has two champions and levelling a clan unlocks the second ([Boss Rush review](https://bossrush.net/2023/03/game-review-race-through-rings-of-hell-in-monster-train/), via search summary).
- Cross unit example [fan]: Umbra Morsel Makers summon Morsels each turn, other units eat them for buffs, and the Penumbra champion on his Glutton path gets extra bonuses from eating Morsels. Players say Umbra units are weak alone and need a lot of floor space. ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/monster-train/best-builds-in-monster-train/); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1102190/discussions/0/3117032860236072824))
- Monster Train 2 (released 21 May 2025, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Train)) adds Room cards installed on a floor for the battle, one room effect per floor (one example boosts all allied units on that floor), and Equipment cards that attach to a unit, one per unit, which can be fused (their costs combine). Dimensional Challenges lock you to preset clan pairs. (via search summary: [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/news/everything-new-in-monster-train-2-revealed-so-far); [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/monster-train-2/engine-ingenuity); [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/04/11/monster-train-2-is-shaping-up-to-be-a-fantastic-sequel/))

**Wildfrost**
- Charms attach to cards between battles; up to 3 per card; they cannot be removed; effects apply at the moment of attachment and are not recalculated, so order matters (on Scrappy Sword, Snowball then Durian removes Snow and adds 4 Attack; the reverse deals 6 damage and keeps Snow). Restrictions: Scrap charms only on Clunkers; Health charms only on Companions and Leaders; a charm cannot add a redundant or conflicting keyword (Pomegranate cannot go on Barrage or Aimless cards). Some charms are tribe exclusive (Acorn: Snowdwellers; Bite: Shademancers; Gear: Clunkmasters). Bells put charms on enemies. Charms do not carry between runs except on an injured Companion from a failed run. Examples: Battle +2 Attack; Heart +5 Health; Snowball applies 1 Snow; Pomegranate grants Barrage and minus 2 Attack; Balance sets Health, Attack and Counter to 3; Mime replaces a card's effects with those of a random Companion in your deck. ([Wildfrost wiki, Charms](https://wildfrostwiki.com/Charms))

**Battle Brothers [fan, Steam forum threads mostly 2016 to 2019]**
- The shieldwall bonus only applies to an adjacent brother also using shieldwall, so one formation alternates shield tanks with two handed axemen inside overlapping walls. Long polearms reach two tiles and stab through the front line from the second row; archers in the second row fire past the front row. Spearwall is strong against zombies and orcs, but human enemies avoid spears. ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/365360/discussions/0/135511027313936007); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/365360/discussions/0/1319962417041958480))

**Fire Emblem**
- Supports: in the GBA games, Path of Radiance and Radiant Dawn, ranked supports work within three spaces; in Awakening and Fates they work through Pair Up, Dual Support and Tag Team, which need adjacency; in Engage they work when adjacent. GBA bonuses sum both units' affinities (Eliwood and Hector at C: +1 Def, +5 Avo, +2 Crit, +5 Ddg). Awakening Dual Strike rate at S support is "([Lead's skill + supporter's skill] / 4) + 60" versus "+ 20" with no support. Caps: GBA units have up to five support levels across partners; Radiant Dawn one ranked support per unit; Awakening one S support per unit. GBA points come from turns spent adjacent. ([Fire Emblem Wiki, Support](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Support))
- Engage: Emblem Rings "allow the character to learn new weapon proficiencies and inherit skills related to the Emblem"; Engage transforms the unit for a few turns and grants a strong single use Engage attack, then a meter refills through combat; "Chain Attacks, which allow certain classes to chip in and help an ally's attack on a nearby foe"; "Chain Guards, which allow certain support classes to completely nullify an attack on an adjoining ally"; units without an Emblem Ring get weaker Bond Rings. ([Wikipedia, Fire Emblem Engage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Emblem_Engage))
- DLC Emblem Bracelets get a stronger "Bond Link" Engage Attack when used next to the Emblem Ring from the same title. ([BreezeWiki mirror of the Fire Emblem fandom Engage page](https://breezewiki.discard.no/fireemblem/wiki/Engage), via search summary)

**Super Auto Pets (strong extra squad example: five slots, auto battle)**
- A team has five slots ([SAP wiki, The Basics](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Basics), via search summary). Battles are automatic and asynchronous; your rightmost pet fights the opponent's leftmost pet and the next pet steps up when one faints; 10 gold per turn; packs include Turtle, Puppy, Star, Golden, Unicorn, Danger and Weekly, and Custom Packs mix pets and food from packs you bought. ([Wikipedia, Super Auto Pets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Auto_Pets))
- Start of battle abilities resolve by attack: "the pet with the most attack goes first". "Nothing that happens in battle is permanent." ([SAP wiki, Glossary](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Glossary))
- Held food perks (one perk slot per pet, implied by Ravioli replacing "a Perk"): Honey (on faint summon a 1/1 Bee), Meat Bone (+3 attack damage), Garlic (take 2 less damage), Melon (take 20 less, once), Mushroom (return as a 1/1 after fainting), Chili (also hit the second enemy for 5), Steak (+20 damage, once). ([SAP wiki, Food](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Food))
- Cross pet example: Panda gives part of its stats to the friend in front, then faints at start of battle; it pairs with Lion and with pets that profit from friends fainting such as Vulture and Shark ([SAP wiki, Panda](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Panda), via search summary).

**Across the Obelisk (party roguelike deckbuilder)**
- "Take sole control of your party of four heroes", or play two to four player online cooperative; "500+ cards, over 300 items and 16 heroes in four different classes"; "Combine character classes in different ways to find your favorite play style." ([Steam, Across the Obelisk](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1385380/Across_the_Obelisk/))
- [fan] One hero's healing scales with Sight and Insanity stacks on enemies while another adds Sight with attacks, so the two pair well. ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1385380/discussions/0/6348431004280107786), via search summary)

### Inferences
- The most portable cross character pattern for Kindred Siege (KS) is **applier plus exploiter** (DD1 Mark, DD2 Combo). It needs no micro from the player, so it suits fighters who act on their own, and it maps straight onto the starting five: Trapper applies, Marksman and Skirmisher exploit, Focus Fire concentrates the payoff. DD1 adds two details worth copying: the marked target becomes a magnet for enemy attacks (counterplay), and the applier has to act first, so in real time the applier needs a faster opening cooldown.
- **Tag counting with tag granting items** (TFT traits plus Emblems) lets one item change the whole squad's bonus. With only five fighters, breakpoints must be small (2 and 3 of a tag). TFT style 2/4/6 breakpoints would need more bodies than KS fields.
- **Line and adjacency items** (Zeke's Herald same row, Sniper's Ring position 4, FE adjacency supports, shieldwall) give KS's Fall Back order a built in price: pulling the lines apart breaks adjacency bonuses. That turns orders and items into one decision instead of two separate systems.
- **Pair bonds** (FE supports, DD2 relationships) fit the "Kindred" name but cost authored content per pair, and DD2's random negative bonds would be hard to read in a fast 2.5D fight. Since army and converts reset each run, bonds would be run scoped unless tied to Pals.
- **Zone items** (Monster Train 2 rooms, one per floor) map to KS lines: one "banner" or station per line that buffs whoever stands in it.
- Determinism (Sim rules: integers, `DeterministicRng`, ordered lists): Super Auto Pets shows a simple fixed trigger order (highest attack first). KS needs an equally fixed order for item triggers, with a tie break by slot index, so replays stay identical.

## 2. How do single character roguelikes turn item pairs into builds?

### Takeaway
Four shapes repeat: a recipe gate (Vampire Survivors: maxed weapon plus a held passive plus a chest), a pair unlock from two sources (Hades duo boons: one qualifying boon from each of two gods), multiplier engines where order and stacking curves matter (Balatro joker order, Risk of Rain 2 proc chains and stacking curves, Slay the Spire relic plus card archetypes), and link or modify systems (Path of Exile support gems, Hades Daedalus hammers, Backpack Battles adjacency recipes). Isaac shows the emergent extreme, with big synergies and items that override each other.

### Cited Findings

**Vampire Survivors**
- Evolution needs a weapon at its required level and its counterpart item, then a Treasure Chest that offers the evolution as its first reward. In most stages that chest comes from bosses at or after the 10 minute mark, with stage exceptions (Dairy Plant, Il Molise, Cappella Magna). An evolution will not appear if the evolved weapon is banished. The base weapon is usually replaced; the passive "remains unaffected". One passive serves several evolutions: Hollow Heart works with Whip, Pako Battiliar and Vanitas Whip; Empty Tome with Magic Wand, Shuriken and Phas3r. Recipes: Whip plus Hollow Heart makes Bloody Tear; Magic Wand plus Empty Tome makes Holy Wand; Garlic plus Pummarola makes Soul Eater; Knife plus Bracer makes Thousand Edge. Unions merge two weapons, for example Peachone plus Ebony Wings makes Vandalier, and "the requisite weapons are merged into one weapon slot", which frees a slot. In some DLC the passive is consumed. ([VS wiki, Evolution](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution))
- The player "cannot be offered new items if the already have 6 different passive items or weapons"; reroll and skip need PowerUps. ([VS wiki, Level up](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Level_up))

**Hades (Supergiant)**
- Each Olympian offers a choice of three boons themed to that god (Zeus lightning, Poseidon knockback waves). ([Wikipedia, Hades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_(video_game)))
- Duo boons: 28 in total; you need only one qualifying boon from each of the two gods. Examples: Curse of Longing (Ares: Curse of Agony or Curse of Pain; Aphrodite: Heartbreak Strike, Heartbreak Flourish, Crush Shot or Passion Dash) makes Doom stack repeatedly on foes afflicted by Weak, each stack 50% harder; Merciful End (Ares curse plus Athena Divine Strike or Flourish) makes each deflect apply 50 extra Doom; Sea Storm (any Poseidon attack boon plus any Zeus attack boon) makes knockaway effects also deal 40 lightning damage; Exclusive Access (Dionysus plus Poseidon) makes every later boon in the run at least Epic. (Cian Maher, 25 Sep 2020) ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/hades-duo-boons-list-how-to-get/))
- Daedalus Hammers change weapon behaviour per aspect; hidden aspects get an exclusive hammer upgrade (Aspect of Arthur: Greater Consecration buffs its Hallowed Ground aura); "It is not possible to gain more than two Daedalus Hammer upgrades before reaching the Temple of Styx"; the Anvil of Fates (275 Obols) removes one hammer upgrade and replaces it with two random ones. ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/hades-best-daedalus-hammer-upgrade-each-weapon/), via search summary)
- Hades II keeps duo boons with more specific requirements, for example Room Temperature needing a Hephaestus Blast boon and a Demeter Freeze boon. ([Game Rant, Hades 2 duos](https://gamerant.com/hades-2-all-legendary-duo-boons-requirements-olypmians-how-get/), via search summary)

**Risk of Rain 2**
- Proc coefficient multiplies an item's trigger chance, effect or duration; skill coefficients are fixed; Luck changes activation odds. Ukulele lightning has coefficient 0.2, AtG Missile Mk. 1 and Charged Perforator 1.0, Sticky Bomb 0.0. Bleed, Burn and Collapse are 0.0 and "will never activate other effects". Railgunner's Supercharged Railgun (3.0) needs only four Tri Tip Daggers to guarantee bleed. ([RoR2 wiki, Proc Coefficient](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Proc_Coefficient))
- Ukulele: 25% chance on hit (multiplied by the hit's proc coefficient) to fire chain lightning at up to 3 targets (plus 2 per stack) within 20 m for 80% damage, at proc coefficient 0.2, so it can set off other on hit items at reduced odds. ([RoR2 wiki, Ukulele](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Ukulele))
- Stacking curves: linear (Soldier's Syringe +15% attack speed per stack, 10 stacks +150%); hyperbolic for chances, 1 minus 1/(1 + a·x), so Tougher Times gives 13.04% block at one stack and 60% at ten, never 100%; exponential (Shaped Glass); special curves (Bandolier). ([RoR2 wiki, Item Stacking](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Item_Stacking))
- Shaped Glass (Lunar): "Increase base damage by 100% (+100% per stack). Reduce maximum health by 50% (+50% per stack)", both exponential; at the 128th stack health rounds to 0 and you die. ([RoR2 wiki, Shaped Glass](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Shaped_Glass))
- A popular mod (ProcSolver) caps proc chain length (3 for chains started by a skill or equipment, 1 for chains started by an item) and credits work on the game's "proc chain mask". This is a mod, not base game behaviour. [fan] ([Thunderstore, ProcSolver](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/RiskOfBrainrot/ProcSolver/))

**The Binding of Isaac**
- Brimstone (Devil Room pool) turns tears into a charged piercing blood laser. Synergies: with Tammy's Head it fires ten beams in all directions; Technology wraps a laser around the beam for a 1.5x damage multiplier; Chocolate Milk lets you release early, 25% damage at minimum charge up to 250% at full; Mom's Knife takes priority and fires a barrage of knives; Ludovico Technique makes a controllable laser ring. Brimstone also overrides many items (Cursed Eye, Evil Eye, Flat Stone and others) and makes Epiphora almost useless. ([Isaac wiki, Brimstone](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Brimstone))
- McMillen's postmortem (28 Nov 2012): "At launch, The Binding of Isaac had 100 items and five playable characters"; "70 percent of the items in Isaac stack, and all the item abilities will affect Isaac in some way"; "Everything about the game was based on complex variables that multiply with each level you pass." ([Game Developer postmortem](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-))

**Slay the Spire (Mega Crit)**
- Anthony Giovannetti (Road to the IGF, 22 Jan 2020): "Allowing for combos and strong synergies is definitely a hallmark of our design process"; "It was important to us that there would not just be a single dominant strategy"; "when building a deck, the player would want to keep multiple things in mind"; "we don't have to worry about the typical downside of strong combos" (a single player game). ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/road-to-the-igf-mega-crit-games-i-slay-the-spire-i-))
- Relic plus archetype example: Dead Branch (Rare): "Whenever you Exhaust a card, add a random card to your hand." The wiki calls Corruption (Skills cost 0 and Exhaust) the classic combo; shiv makers like Blade Dance and Storm of Steel also feed it. ([StS wiki, Dead Branch](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_Branch))

**Balatro**
- Most runs start with 5 Joker slots. Position matters for some Jokers: Blueprint "Copies ability of Joker to the right"; Brainstorm "Copies the ability of leftmost Joker"; Ceremonial Dagger destroys the Joker to its right. Baron: "Each King held in hand gives X1.5 Mult"; Mime: "Retrigger all card held in hand abilities". Jokers are tagged +Mult, XMult, Chips or Effect. Editions: Foil +50 Chips, Holographic +10 Mult, Polychrome X1.5 Mult, Negative +1 Joker slot. Rarity odds: Common 70%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 5%; Legendaries only from The Soul. ([Balatro wiki, Jokers](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers))

**Backpack Battles**
- Items have shapes and interact with neighbours (the Pan deals more damage next to food). "For two items to combine, they must be adjacent to each other"; combining happens "at the end of the battle phase but before the shop phase"; two Whetstones next to a Hero Sword make the Hero Longsword. Battles are automatic and asynchronous against other players' runs; you win with 10 wins before 5 losses. Early access began 8 Mar 2024; 640,000 units sold on Steam in the first month. ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles))

**Path of Exile**
- Skill gems sit in sockets on gear; "Active skills can be modified by items known as Support Gems"; what a support adds depends on linked sockets; "Given limits on the number of sockets, players must prioritize gem usage." The passive tree has 2,268 nodes shared by all classes. 3.23 added Transfigured Gems, alternative versions of skill gems. ([Wikipedia, Path of Exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path_of_Exile))
- In Path of Exile 2, a character may only use a single copy of each Lineage support gem (Solus Ipse bypasses this). ([PoE2 wiki, Lineage](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Lineage), via search summary)

### Inferences
- **Hades maps almost one to one onto KS.** Hades boons attach to verbs (attack, special, cast, dash, call); KS has four order verbs (Hold, Unleash, Fall Back, Focus Fire) and four Pals. Pal favours that change an order, plus one duo favour per Pal pair, give 6 duos from 4 Pals. Hades' 28 duos equal the number of pairs among 8 Olympian gods (8 choose 2 = 28), so Hades appears to author exactly one duo per pair (LIKELY, the god count is my knowledge, the 28 is sourced). Complete pair coverage is cheap when the set is small. This fits the lock "orders come from the leading Pal's god" and the Shade "upgrades unlock orders". Clash: the 10 Oct lock keeps Pals at camp, so in run favours need a fiction or must be bought at camp. Jacob's call.
- **Vampire Survivors' recipe gate suits chief breaks.** "Max a fighter's gear plus hold trinket X anywhere in the squad, then break a chief" mirrors "max weapon plus passive plus boss chest after 10 minutes". Reusing one trinket across several classes' evolutions (the Hollow Heart pattern) keeps the trinket pool small as classes grow. Unions that merge two items into one slot are a strong reward when slots are scarce.
- **Hammers** (behaviour changers, max two before the last region, one reroll shop) suit class signature gear: "Marksman shots pierce", "Warden Hold reflects". They also suit modifying the chief order each chief gives for the rest of the run.
- **Risk of Rain 2 trigger chains** port well to on event gear (on parry, on Unleash, on kill) if KS uses integer chances times an integer per skill proc coefficient, gives damage over time a coefficient of 0 so it cannot chain (RoR2 already does this for Bleed and Burn), caps chain depth, and uses hyperbolic curves for chance stats so they never reach 100%. All of this has to stay integer and run through `DeterministicRng` to obey the Sim rules. Readability is the risk: fast chains are hard to see in 2.5D.
- **Isaac** proves that visible stacking items create memorable builds, and that fits the lock "gear enhances a unit's look", as long as it stays gear and not mutation. Its overrides (Brimstone cancelling other tear items) are a natural brake, but hidden item info clashes with KS's codex priority.
- **Balatro's five ordered Joker slots** look like KS's five fighters in a line. Copy gear ("echo the gear of the fighter to your right") and order sensitive +Mult then XMult style stacking make line order a build axis without new content.
- **Backpack Battles adjacency recipes** suggest gear that combines when the two holders stand next to each other at deploy. Movement breaks adjacency in real time, so it should be checked at deploy or at a chief break, not every frame.
- **Path of Exile tags** scale better than pair content: an order mod tagged "Area" works on any Area skill, so 17 classes do not need 136 hand made pair combos. The PoE2 one copy rule is a clean brake for squad wide gear.

## 3. How do these games teach combos so a new player finds them?

### Takeaway
The proven teaching ladder is: make statuses visible on the unit (DD2 tokens), show the other half of a recipe when the player holds one half (Vampire Survivors level up info, TFT recipe list, Backpack Battles lines that light up), only offer the combo once its prerequisites are met (Hades duos, VS chest evolutions), keep a permanent collection with blank entries and unlock hints (Balatro, Risk of Rain 2 logbook), and drip new pieces in through unlock levels (Monster Train clan levels, TFT Set 1 champion additions). Isaac is the deliberate exception that leaves discovery to experiment and community.

### Cited Findings
- **Vampire Survivors:** the evolution replaces the chest's usual random upgrade as its first reward. Level up panels were updated (Mar 2022) to show "info for discovered evolutions", and "level up evo indicators" were fixed for DLC weapons (Jun 2023). ([VS wiki, Evolution](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution); [VS wiki, Level up](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Level_up))
- **Hades:** a duo boon only has a chance to appear once you hold one qualifying boon from each of its two gods ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/hades-duo-boons-list-how-to-get/)). Players report the Codex shows exact prerequisites once unlocked [fan] ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145350/discussions/0/4697909730619656492)). Raising a god's Keepsake rank improves the odds of that god's duos, and the God's Legacy mirror talent raises the chance of Legendary or Duo boons ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-what-are-duo-boons-how-get-duo-boons), via search summary; direct fetch was blocked).
- **TFT:** components can be crafted from "the item bench recipe list" ([LoL wiki, TFT:Item](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Item)). Riot names "discovery" a pillar; in Set 1 champions were added gradually, "about one or two every patch or two", up to around 60 ([Riot dev blog](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-design-pillars-of-tft/)).
- **Balatro:** 105 Jokers are available from the first run and 45 must be unlocked; "Available Jokers are shown as Not Discovered" in the Collection until obtained once in an unseeded run; locked entries usually show their unlock condition and progress; Legendaries appear as "?????". ([Balatro wiki, Jokers](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers))
- **Risk of Rain 2:** picking up an item unlocks its logbook entry; some items must be unlocked by completing a Challenge before they can appear. ([RoR2 wiki, Items](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Items))
- **Backpack Battles:** guides report that the line between recipe ingredients turns from blue to gold or yellow once a recipe is set up. ([GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/backpack-battles-all-recipes-listed/); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/all-recipes-crafting-combinations-in-backpack-battles), via search summary)
- **Binding of Isaac:** McMillen, on the Zelda influence: "You weren't sure what things did until you experimented with them"; he wanted players to feel "like the game was endless and alive". ([Game Developer postmortem](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-))
- **Slay the Spire:** Giovannetti: "The important thing is to quickly allow the player to start carving out new strategies"; "There are so many different permutations of decks that you can build". ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/road-to-the-igf-mega-crit-games-i-slay-the-spire-i-))
- **Monster Train:** "Each clan has 10 levels to unlock, bringing new cards to your deck". ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1102190/Monster_Train/))
- **Darkest Dungeon 2:** tokens are visible below every combatant's health bar ([DD wiki, Tokens](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens)); relationships come with act outs and barks during combat that show the bond working ([DD wiki, Relationships](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Relationships)).
- **Fire Emblem Engage:** bond levels with an Emblem unlock conversations and battle bonuses ([Wikipedia, Fire Emblem Engage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Emblem_Engage)); support levels unlock conversations and "increased bonuses when fighting nearby in battle" (same source).
- **Wildfrost:** invalid charm combos are blocked at attach time (no redundant or conflicting keywords), which stops players from wasting a charm on a dead combo; but order of attachment silently changes the result, a known trap. ([Wildfrost wiki, Charms](https://wildfrostwiki.com/Charms))

### Inferences
- KS's tribes codex is already permanent across runs and a top priority, so it is the natural recipe book: a "Kindred combos" page with blank silhouettes and unlock hints (Balatro), filled in when a combo first fires (RoR2 logbook).
- The pre fight card that says what a tribe learned (call C7 in DESIGN_LOCKS) can also hint at a combo that beats that habit. That joins discovery and anti dominance in one screen.
- When a fighter holds half a recipe, show the missing half on reward and forge screens (VS level up info, TFT recipe list). When both halves are in the squad, light the link between the two fighters (Backpack Battles gold line).
- Gate the first duo or evolution so it lands at a chief break, which is already the big moment in the KS loop (VS chest moment).
- Drip combos in with class unlocks (Monster Train clan levels), so the first runs teach the starting five's pairs before new classes add more.
- Keep a few secret combos for community discovery (Isaac), but not the core ones, since KS's audience target is "an absurd, addictive loop that players can beat in many ways".

## 4. How do they stop a single dominant build?

### Takeaway
No game relies on one brake. The proven mix is: random offers with limited control (VS rerolls and banishes, Hades duos never guaranteed, StS card choices), hard caps and one copy rules (VS 6 slots, TFT 3 items, SAP one perk, MT2 one equipment, Wildfrost 3 charms, PoE sockets), power with a built in cost (DD1 trinket penalties, Shaped Glass, Wildfrost Pomegranate), counters that are shown and temporary (Balatro boss blinds, StS bosses, DD1 tribe resistances), contested resources (TFT shared pool), and live balancing from data (Mega Crit metrics, TFT set rotation). Balatro's The Ox, which punishes your most played hand, is a direct precedent for KS tribes learning your habits.

### Cited Findings
- **Slay the Spire:** "It was important to us that there would not just be a single dominant strategy." ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/road-to-the-igf-mega-crit-games-i-slay-the-spire-i-)) Giovannetti's GDC 2019 talk "Slay the Spire: Metrics Driven Design and Balance" covers a metric driven focus from early development ([GDC news](https://gdconf.com/news/learn-slay-spires-successful-metrics-driven-approach-game-balancing-gdc-2019); [GDC Vault](https://www.gdcvault.com/browse/gdc-19/play/1025731)); an attendee summary reports the goals "every card should have a place" and "avoiding anything too warping" [secondary] ([Mechanics of Magic](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2022/05/22/critical-play-is-this-game-balanced-10/)). Time Eater's Time Warp: "Whenever you play a certain number of cards, ends your turn and gains X Strength", which punishes card spam turns. ([StS wiki, Time Eater](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Time_Eater))
- **Balatro boss blinds:** The Ox: playing your most played hand type sets your money to $0; The Eye: no repeat hand types this round; The Mouth: only 1 hand type this round; The Pillar: cards played earlier this Ante are debuffed; suit debuffs (The Club, The Goad, The Window, The Head); The Plant debuffs face cards; The Flint halves base chips and mult; finals: Crimson Heart disables one random Joker every hand, Verdant Leaf debuffs all cards until you sell a Joker, Amber Acorn flips and shuffles all Jokers. ([Balatro wiki, Boss Blinds](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Boss_Blinds)) At higher stakes Jokers can carry Eternal ("Can't be sold or destroyed"), Perishable ("Debuffed after 5 rounds") or Rental ($3 per round) stickers. ([Balatro wiki, Jokers](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers))
- **TFT:** a beta era report says shared pool sizes meant only one player could make the same 3 star champion of tier 4 or 5, and only two players the same tier 3 [secondary, beta numbers] ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/8481/tft-champion-pool-sizes-and-probabilities-revealed), via search summary). ### Inferences
- KS's rivalry engine is a stronger form of Balatro's The Ox and StS's archetype bosses. They work best when the counter is visible before the fight (my own knowledge, not verified in this pass: StS shows the act boss on the map and Balatro previews the boss blind), and they tax a build rather than delete it (a debuffed suit lasts one blind). KS already shows what the tribe learned on the fight card; the counter should then cut a favourite's value, not ban it, so players keep favourites.
- Give the player one pivot tool against a counter: a reroll like Director's Cut, or a swap like the Anvil of Fates, bought with Pal materials.
- Use caps that fit five fighters: one or two gear slots per fighter, one perk slot (SAP), one copy per squad for squad wide gear (PoE2 Lineage), and a hard cap on behaviour changers (Hades: two hammers).
- Put the biggest power on tradeoff gear: Shaped Glass, Fanged Spear Tip, or KS specific costs such as faster own nerve drain (KS already makes Hold drain your own nerve).
- KS has bots that play the game (the reading bot and the plain bot in DESIGN_LOCKS). Logging which combos win and how often they get picked gives Mega Crit style metrics without a live player base.
- Avoid the Backpack Battles early access trap: a recipe system needs enough items that runs differ, so the starting five should ship with enough combos per pair before more classes arrive.

## 5. Which mechanisms work when the player starts with a small fixed squad (five fighters) and only unlocks more classes later?

### Takeaway
The games that start small and grow do three things: they make the starting set complete enough to show builds on day one, they add new pieces in visible batches tied to progress (Monster Train clan levels, Balatro unlocks, RoR2 challenges, TFT gradual champions), and they link pieces through shared tags or statuses rather than hand made pair content, saving per pair content for small sets (Hades has 28 duos, which matches 8 gods taken in pairs). With 17 classes there are 136 class pairs, so KS should link classes through statuses and tags and keep hand authored pair combos for the four Pals (six pairs).

### Cited Findings
- **Monster Train:** "Each clan has 10 levels to unlock, bringing new cards to your deck"; 10 starter decks; five clans. ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1102190/Monster_Train/)) Levelling a clan unlocks its second champion. ([Boss Rush review](https://bossrush.net/2023/03/game-review-race-through-rings-of-hell-in-monster-train/), via search summary) Monster Train 2 Dimensional Challenges lock players to preset clan pairs. (via search summary, [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/news/everything-new-in-monster-train-2-revealed-so-far))
- **Balatro:** 105 Jokers available from the first run, 45 unlocked by conditions shown with progress. ([Balatro wiki, Jokers](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers))
- **Risk of Rain 2:** some items only appear after their Challenge is completed. ([RoR2 wiki, Items](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Items))
- **TFT Set 1:** champions were added gradually, one or two every patch or two, up to about 60. ([Riot dev blog](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-design-pillars-of-tft/))
- **Super Auto Pets:** five team slots ([SAP wiki, The Basics](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Basics), via search summary); packs including a Weekly pack, and custom packs drawn from owned packs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Auto_Pets)).
- **Darkest Dungeon 1:** two trinket slots per hero; each class has 1 to 3 Common or Uncommon class trinkets and one Rare or Very Rare one. ([DD wiki, Trinkets](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets))
- **Across the Obelisk:** a party of four drawn from 16 heroes in four classes. ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1385380/Across_the_Obelisk/))
- **Fire Emblem:** support caps per unit (GBA five support levels across partners; Radiant Dawn one ranked support; Awakening one S support). ([Fire Emblem Wiki, Support](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Support))
- **Wildfrost:** some charms are tribe exclusive. ([Wildfrost wiki, Charms](https://wildfrostwiki.com/Charms))
- **Vampire Survivors:** one passive serves several weapons (Hollow Heart: Whip, Pako Battiliar, Vanitas Whip), so new weapons can reuse old passives. ([VS wiki, Evolution](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution))
- **Hades:** 28 duo boons. ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/hades-duo-boons-list-how-to-get/))
- **Isaac:** 100 items at launch; the expansion added "new items that shuffle into the mix". ([Game Developer postmortem](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mcmillen-and-himsl-s-i-the-binding-of-isaac-i-))

### Inferences
- Pair maths (mine): 5 starting classes give 10 pairs; 17 classes give 136 pairs; choosing 5 different classes from 17 gives 6,188 squads. 4 Pals give 6 pairs. Hand making a combo for every class pair (the Fire Emblem support approach) stops scaling well past the starting five. Hades' complete pair coverage works because its set is small, which matches the 4 Pals.
- Make the starting five complete first: each of the 10 starter pairs should have at least one item or status link (for example Trapper snare to Marksman payoff, Warden Hold to Medic heal, Skirmisher opening token to Warden finisher). That follows Mega Crit's "quickly allow the player to start carving out new strategies".
- Each class unlock should bring one new status or tag and gear that bridges it to at least two starter classes (the VS reused passive pattern), so a new class plugs into existing builds instead of needing its own set.
- Link classes through statuses and tags (DD1 Mark, TFT traits, PoE tags) so any applier works with any exploiter. Then the rivalry engine can learn to resist a status or tag rather than a class, and a newly unlocked class is not countered on arrival. DESIGN_LOCKS already says class progression must account for rivalry adaptation.
- Release gear and combo unlocks in visible batches with progress shown in the codex (Balatro, Monster Train clan levels). Offer curated challenge squads (MT2 Dimensional Challenges) to teach each newly unlocked class.
- With five fixed slots, line position becomes free build variety (SAP order, Balatro joker order, DD1 position trinkets, FE adjacency), and costs no new content.
- Converts already arrive with gear ("Converts keep their look and add the cult sigil and their Pal's gear"). TFT's carousel, which puts "an item on each champion", shows that bundling a unit with an item makes recruitment a build decision, not only a headcount one.

### Gaps
- No source was found on how Across the Obelisk or Darkest Dungeon gate new heroes behind progression, or on unlock pacing data (how many combos per unlock keeps players engaged).
- No source in this pass covers a game where enemies adapt to the player's item builds over many runs, which is KS's rivalry engine. Balatro's The Ox and StS bosses are static counters, not learning ones.

## 1. Items: what gear exists, how big the pool is, and what an item can change

### Takeaway
Run gear is one slot per fighter, one stat per piece, three tiers: Pauper +5, Mid +10, Princess +15, on damage %, health %, Hit or Avoid. Only the four chief Princess pieces carry a special, and one camp forged item (the Lava Sun Tabard) exists outside the gear slot. "Sets" are not gear sets: they come from how many fighters of the same tribe stand in the squad. Items change numbers, with five small behaviour hooks at most (kill nerve, big hit guard, bleed, bonus on the wounded, fear).

### Inferences
- Inferred pool size: 4 tribes x 4 stats x 3 tiers = 48 plain combinations (Princess tier spoils included, all with no special), plus 4 chief Princess pieces with specials, plus the tabard. That is 4 stat effects, 4 specials and 1 fear bonus in total.
- Inferred: the Clarth chief's Princess piece (Avoid, no special) is defined but unreachable, since the Clarth chief is never fought.
- Inferred: the Skullpeak piece's guard halves Eclipse and Firebomb hits only. It does nothing against Dust Shark, Tide, Blood Call or heavy swings, despite its card text.

- Inferred: nothing an item does touches targeting, orders, combos, cast timing or class actions. The only "build" levers today are which tribes are in the squad (traits and 2/3 sets), the random fixed quirk, one stat piece, the tabard and the run's favours (section 3).

## 2. Item drops: how a player gets items, materials and upgrades today

### Takeaway
Every won road fight banks materials at once and offers one thing in their place until the next fight: a gear piece (strip or bank) or, on the row's favour fight, one of a Pal's favours. Chiefs give either their whole band and order (capture) or their Princess piece (kill). The only shop is the Bone Trader event. The only camp spend is one forge recipe (the tabard, 50 materials). All drops are deterministic from seeds; there is no loot table.

### Cited Findings
- Materials per won road fight 10, per chief or hunt 30 ([CampaignRules.cs:654-655](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignRules.cs#L654), [Campaign.cs:1403](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L1403)); an ambush pays only the hunter's rank reward, 50 / 30 / 20 / 15 for rank 1 / 2-3 / 4-6 / 7+ ([HunterCompany.cs:504-507](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/HunterCompany.cs#L504)); each omen picked adds 25% ([Campaign.cs:1406](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L1406), [CampaignRoad.cs:377](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignRoad.cs#L377)). - Inferred: in practice a Princess piece with a special comes only from killing a chief. A Princess tier piece from a raised Mid drop (trial or duel plus objective, or two omens) is just +15 with no special.

- Inferred: strip versus bank is a trade of materials (camp progress) against run power plus bounty heat; favours make the same trade without heat.
- Inferred: materials have three sinks today: the tabard (once), event costs, and paying back banked materials when stripping or taking a favour.

### Gaps
- Does not exist yet: treasure or chest nodes, a merchant node or camp shop beyond the Bone Trader event, selling or scrapping items, rerolls, a choice between several drops, rarity weighting, Glutton / Shade / Whisperer camp upgrades or recipes, a second forge recipe.

## 4. Run stages: road nodes, encounter kinds, events, objectives, omens, ambushes, hunters, finale

### Takeaway
A run is camp, then a tier 1 road (Bloodridge or Stoneveil), its chief, a tier 2 road (Skullpeak or Thundercoast), its chief, then the hunters' finale. Each road is a 3 lane by 3 row map with two node kinds (fight, event), four road fight kinds, three placeholder events, three objective types and four omens. The hunters (the rival tribe) add ambush nodes from bounty 2 and lead the finale. A known Sim bug made only two of the four tier routes reachable; Codex was fixing it during this read.

### Cited Findings
- Tiers in code: Clarth (starter) tier 1, Bloodridge 1, Stoneveil 1, Skullpeak 2, Thundercoast 2 ([CampaignRules.cs:371-593](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/CampaignRules.cs#L371)); a run meets one tribe per tier at random, then the hunters ([Campaign.cs:817-848](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L817); lock [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:395, 405](DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L395)). The hunters fight at tier 3 strength and are not on the path ([AlphaHunt.cs:22-31](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/AlphaHunt.cs#L22)).
- Tier pick bug: the raw FNV hash forced opposite picks, so only Bloodridge then Thundercoast, or Stoneveil then Skullpeak, could happen ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:9717-9718, 9756](HANDOFF_LOG.md#L9717)). Codex claimed the fix on 19 Oct ([HANDOFF_LOG.md:9761-9763](HANDOFF_LOG.md#L9761)); Campaign.cs now has an avalanche finalised `TierPick` plus a legacy flag for runs in progress ([Campaign.cs:828-866](Assets/Scripts/Sim/Learning/Campaign.cs#L828)). ## 8. Codex and discovery

### Takeaway

# Player agency during automatic combat: auto battlers, tower defence and real time with pause squad games

Notes for the report writer. Context: a Unity roguelike where a squad of 5 fights on its own and the player has 4 orders (Hold, which is also a timed parry; Fall Back, a dodge; Focus Fire on one target; Unleash, a burst and scare) plus one chief spell paid with points earned in the fight. In a real playtest the player pressed only 4 orders in a 41 second fight and said he did not know what was going on.

Source quality key: wiki numbers come from community wikis (fandom or wiki.gg) read through their page source, and can lag behind patches. Anything I worked out myself is in the Inferences lists, not in Cited Findings. Per the copyright rule I paraphrase sources and keep one short direct quote in the whole file.

## 1. Kingdom Rush (all entries, especially Vengeance and Alliance): what the player does every few seconds

### Takeaway
Kingdom Rush keeps a hands on layer on top of automatic towers: a cheap, fast recharging Reinforcements drop (10 to 15 s) that the wiki says there is little reason not to spam, one or two slow aimed area spells (Rain of Fire 60 to 80 s, or hero spells mostly at 25 to 80 s, with outliers from 18 to 200 s), hero movement by tap or drag, rally points, tower builds and upgrades, and an early wave call that pays gold and cuts spell cooldowns. Ironhide said it built this on purpose so the player is not just planning and then watching.

### Cited Findings
**Original Kingdom Rush (2011) and Frontiers**
- Rain of Fire: picking it shows a red target marker on the map. The player picks a spot and several meteors fall, dealing area true damage to ground and flying enemies. It starts with 3 meteors. The wiki calls it good for small hordes and a last moment lifesaver, held back by its long cooldown ([KR Wiki: Rain of Fire](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_of_Fire))
- Rain of Fire cooldown in Kingdom Rush by upgrade level: 80 s at levels 0 to 2, 70 s at level 3, 60 s at levels 4 and 5. Meteors go from 3 to 5 to 10, and later levels leave burning ground ([KR Wiki: Rain of Fire](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_of_Fire))
- Frontiers Rain of Fire cooldown: 80, 75, 75, 70, 70, 60 s by level (the old Flash version was 120 down to 110 s) ([KR Wiki: Rain of Fire/Frontiers](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_of_Fire/Frontiers))
- Call Reinforcements: summons two soldiers that act like barracks troops and block ground enemies. Lifetime 20 s, recharge 10 s. The wiki says the quick recharge means there is little sense in not using it, and lists its jobs: stand in for barracks, seal or reinforce choke points, hold enemies near the exit. Upgrades take the soldiers from 30 HP farmers to 110 HP legionnaires with a spear throw that can hit flyers ([KR Wiki: Call Reinforcements](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Call_Reinforcements))
- Hotkeys are 1 for Rain of Fire and 2 for Reinforcements, Space cancels a picked spell, power or rally point, and P pauses. Guide tips: aim Rain of Fire a little ahead of the target, use Reinforcements to split enemy groups and to slow them with frequent drops, and move barracks rally points to good spots ([Ayumilove KR guide](https://ayumilove.net/kingdom-rush-walkthrough-guide/))
- Calling the next enemy wave early pays bonus money and lowers spell cooldowns ([Ayumilove KR guide](https://ayumilove.net/kingdom-rush-walkthrough-guide/))
- Heroes are single units the player directs around the level. Their special moves grow stronger as they earn experience during a level, and they come back a while after being defeated ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush))

**Origins (2014)**
- Thunderbolt replaces Rain of Fire. The player picks it and taps anywhere on the enemy path, and 3 bolts strike at once. It is more accurate than Rain of Fire because the bolts land instantly instead of taking a few seconds to fall. Cooldown 70 s, dropping to 60 s with upgrades. The top upgrade slows every enemy on the map while it fires ([KR Wiki: Thunderbolt](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Thunderbolt))
- Origins Reinforcements: recharge rises to 15 s, returning to 10 s only with the tier 4 upgrade Rapid Response. Lifetime stays 20 s ([KR Wiki: Call Reinforcements/Origins](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Call_Reinforcements/Origins))
- Origins adds a Hero Spell: each hero has one active spell, usable without spending upgrade points. Cooldowns: Phoenix egg 18 s, Catha stun 25 s, Xin 30 s, Lynn 30 s, Eridan arrow storm 40 s, Bravebark 40 s, Wilbur 40 s, Faustus 40 s, Durax 45 s, Bruce 45 s, Lilith 50 s, Razz and Rags 60 s, Prince Denas 60 s, Arivan 80 s, Vez'nan 120 s, Reg'son 200 s falling to 50 s with upgrades ([KR Wiki: Hero Spell](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_Spell))

**Vengeance (2018)**
- Soul Impact replaces Rain of Fire. The wiki says it is not accurate, so it has to be placed right on the enemy. Cooldown 70 s, and the wiki says the long cooldown limits it until upgraded ([KR Wiki: Soul Impact](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Impact))
- Vengeance Reinforcements (Demon Goonies) recharge in 14 s, with lifetimes of 12 to 18 s by upgrade ([KR Wiki: Call Reinforcements/Vengeance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Call_Reinforcements/Vengeance))
- The third spell is set by the chosen hero, an Ultimate Spell (renamed from Hero Spell). Heroes are player controlled to a degree, use their other skills on their own, and revive after a delay ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush: Vengeance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush:_Vengeance)). Example: Veruk's ultimate calls two warmongers for 10 s on a 40 s cooldown ([KR Wiki: Ultimate Spell](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Spell))

**Alliance (Kingdom Rush 5, released 25 July 2024)**
- The player controls two heroes at once for the first time. There are three spells: classic two soldier Reinforcements plus one ultimate from each of the two heroes. There is no big area spell like Rain of Fire, Thunderbolt or Soul Impact. Five towers are brought to each stage and each upgrades four times. On mobile, gem bought items (freezes, stalls) add more manual verbs; Steam has no items ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush_5:_Alliance))
- Also new in Alliance: units can be moved by click dragging, and enemy intro cards can be reopened during play by tapping the enemy icons ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush_5:_Alliance))
- Alliance Reinforcements recharge in 15 s, or 12 s with the Call to Action upgrade. Lifetime 12 to 15 s. The tree splits at level 3 into tougher blockers or ranged archers ([KR Wiki: Call Reinforcements/Alliance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Call_Reinforcements/Alliance))
- Alliance ultimate cooldowns from the wiki: Lumenir 30 s; Stregi, Spydyr, Broden and Sun Wukong 35 s (the Sun Wukong entry also gives 53 s, so the wiki contradicts itself); Therien, Onagro, Kosmyr, Bonehart and Sylvara 45 s; Vesper, Grimson, Torres, Anya and Warhead 50 s; Raelyn and Kratoa 60 s; Aurion 78 s. Most are described as hitting an area or a spot on the path (arrow storms, a steel ball dropped on the path, lava bursts, root fields), which fits the series' tap to target spells; the page does not spell out the targeting step ([KR Wiki: Ultimate Spell/Alliance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Spell/Alliance)). Arivan's Elemental Storm is listed at 80 s in a search snippet of his page ([KR Wiki: Arivan](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Arivan))
- Alliance hero upgrades that touch pacing: Unlimited Vigor cuts all hero spell cooldowns by 10%, and Limit Pushing instantly resets a hero spell's cooldown after every fifth use ([KR Wiki: Template KR5A Hero Upgrade](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Template:KR5A_Hero_Upgrade)). Vengeance had a hero upgrade cutting ultimate cooldowns by 20% ([KR Wiki: Template KRV Hero Upgrade](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Template:KRV_Hero_Upgrade))
- Hero control in Alliance: tap the hero and then a destination, or tap the hero icon and then any path, or drag from the hero in the direction of travel. Heroes stay on the path except fliers and teleporters. Listed respawn time is 30 s for the heroes in the table I read ([KR Wiki: Heroes/Alliance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Heroes/Alliance))

**Genesis (Kingdom Rush 6, announced 26 February 2026, released 24 September 2026)**
- Spells are no longer fixed or tied to heroes. Before a stage the player picks any three of the unlocked spells, and spells earn experience and level up as they are used. Two heroes per stage ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush 6: Genesis](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush_6:_Genesis))
- Genesis spell cooldowns: Reinforcements 15 s, Ace Musketeers 20 s, Wintersong's Wrath 40 s, Teleportation Sigil 45 s, Royal Edict 50 s, Gnome's Shop 50 s, Thunder Zapper 50 s, Rain of Fire 60 s, Aspect of Sol 60 s. Every cast earns the spell XP, and the XP is kept whether the battle is won, lost or restarted. Cooldowns are halved in the Blitz Challenge and Spell Rush modes ([Wikily: KR6 spells](https://wikily.gg/kingdom-rush-6-genesis/spells))
- A search snippet of a Genesis spell page gives Reinforcements a 12 s soldier duration ([Wikily: KR6 Reinforcements](https://wikily.gg/kingdom-rush-6-genesis/spells/reinforcements))

**What Ironhide and reviewers say**
- Ironhide cofounder Alvaro Azofra said most tower defence games have the player set the strategy and then sit back and watch with little intervention. Ironhide wanted the opposite: "we wanted to make the player feel involved, busy", with each stage feeling like a battle in progress, and with more action than the genre usually had ([Kodeco interview](https://www.kodeco.com/2769-making-a-hit-tower-defense-game-a-top-app-dev-interview))
- Azofra in a Spanish interview: a designer hand picks which enemies come and when, because algorithm made waves get learned fast, lose surprise and feel mechanical. With 15 waves per screen, each screen takes many days of trial and error ([ENTER.CO interview](https://www.enter.co/especiales/videojuegos/la-ciencia-y-el-arte-detras-de-un-buen-tower-defense-entrevista/))
- Juan Fernández, lead designer of Alliance and Genesis, said Genesis keeps the core gameplay and adds upgrade trees for heroes and spells, a short intense Blitz mode, a Spell Rush mode with no heroes, and a Classic mode with one hero ([Pocket Gamer KR6 interview](https://www.pocketgamer.com/kingdom-rush-6-genesis/dev-interview/))
- Pocket Gamer's review of the first game describes the core as racing to stay ahead of the waves, with the power ups (bonus troops and fireballs) built to be dropped in at the last minute to plug holes in the line. The reviewer says the humour keeps a tough and unusually hands on tower defence game from feeling overwhelming or tedious (paraphrase) ([Pocket Gamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/036437/r/))

### Inferences
- Inferred: in any 41 second stretch of Kingdom Rush the player can cast Reinforcements about 3 or 4 times (10 to 15 s recharge), may fire one long cooldown spell or ultimate, and also moves one or two heroes, sets rally points and buys or upgrades towers. That is roughly 6 to 10 inputs, against the 4 seen in the Kindred Siege playtest.
- Inferred: Kingdom Rush gives its verbs layers. One cheap, short cooldown, low stakes verb you are told to spam (Reinforcements), a few medium cooldown aimed verbs (hero ultimates at 25 to 60 s), and one rare big panic button (Rain of Fire style at 60 to 80 s). Kindred Siege's four orders plus chief spell could be tuned into similar layers, with at least one order cheap and always useful.
- Inferred: aimed spells with travel time (Rain of Fire) ask the player to read where enemies will be. Ironhide moved to instant or on target versions later (Thunderbolt is instant, Soul Impact must sit right on the enemy), which suggests they cut aiming skill in favour of timing and placement.
- Inferred: Genesis rewarding every cast with XP, win or lose, is a direct push against hoarding, the pattern seen in the playtest.

### Gaps
- No Ironhide postmortem or GDC talk with measured input rates or playtest data turned up. The TouchArcade review of the first game would not load.
- I did not get exact Frontiers Reinforcements figures or the full Vengeance ultimate list.
- Hero respawn times outside the four Alliance heroes in the table were not collected.

## 2. Clash Royale: the elixir economy, decision rhythm and readability

### Takeaway
Clash Royale gives the player one shared, slowly refilling resource (1 elixir per 2.8 s, start 5, cap 10) and a hand of 4 cards from an 8 card deck. Every card play is a choice of what, where and when, usually answering a play the opponent just made, and the elixir trade tells you whether it worked. Over a 3 minute match that is roughly one play every 7 to 8 seconds, speeding up as elixir doubles and triples. Its Champion cards are the closest live match to a chief spell: an auto fighting unit whose ability the player fires by paying elixir, behind a 1 s delay and a 12 to 30 s cooldown.

### Cited Findings
- A battle starts with 5 elixir and holds at most 10. Elixir comes at 1 per 2.8 s, every 1.4 s in double elixir, and about every 0.9 s in triple. After 2 minutes production doubles for 2 more minutes, and after 4 minutes it triples ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir))
- Regular time is 3 minutes. Elixir doubles for the last minute. A tie goes to a 2 minute sudden death overtime whose last minute is triple elixir, and then to a tiebreaker ([CR Wiki: Basics of Battle](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Basics_of_Battle)). Supercell's support page, seen only as a search snippet, gives the same schedule: x1 for two minutes, x2 in the third, then x2 and x3 in overtime ([Clash Royale support: Elixir](https://support.clashroyale.com/hc/en-us/articles/49484936933403-Elixir))
- A 3 minute battle with no overtime and no waste gives about 90.7 elixir in total ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir))
- In 2v2, elixir comes 30% slower (about 3.6, 1.8 and 1.2 s) to make up for the number of cards that would otherwise be played ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir))
- In battle 4 of the 8 deck cards are in hand, with a fifth shown in a Next slot. A card entering Next waits 2 s before it can enter the hand (1 s in double elixir) ([CR Wiki: Basics of Battle](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Basics_of_Battle)). The deck screen shows the average elixir cost, and the wiki uses 3.5 as an example of a deck that can play cards often ([CR Wiki: Basics of Battle](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Basics_of_Battle))
- A November 2019 update cut overtime to 2 minutes (from 3), made the last minute triple elixir, and dealt cards into the hand faster ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir); [CR Wiki: Basics of Battle](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Basics_of_Battle))
- Elixir trades: you profit when your answer costs less than the attack it stops (the wiki lists +2 trades such as Arrows against a Minion Horde). The defender usually has the edge because the towers help defend for free. The wiki warns against sitting at 10 (leaking) and against dropping to 0, which leaves you open to a push ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir); [CR Wiki: Basics of Battle](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Basics_of_Battle))
- When the bar fills, the game shows a full bar text warning and makes the cards in hand shake ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir))
- Champion abilities use a button on the right, above the cards. Every one fires after a 1 s delay, costs elixir and goes on cooldown after its effect ends:
  - Archer Queen, Cloaking Cape: 1 elixir, invisible with 80% faster attacks for 3.5 s, then a 17 s cooldown ([CR Wiki: Archer Queen](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Archer_Queen))
  - Golden Knight, Dashing Dash: 1 elixir, 12 s cooldown ([CR Wiki: Golden Knight](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Knight))
  - Monk, Pensive Protection: 1 elixir, takes 65% less damage and reflects projectiles, 17 s cooldown. The wiki calls reactive use against spells high risk and high reward, with a very small window to time it ([CR Wiki: Monk](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Monk))
  - Mighty Miner: 1 elixir, 13 s cooldown ([CR Wiki: Mighty Miner](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Mighty_Miner)); Skeleton King: 2 elixir, 20 s cooldown ([CR Wiki: Skeleton King](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Skeleton_King)); Little Prince: 3 elixir, 30 s cooldown ([CR Wiki: Little Prince](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Little_Prince))
  - Trade maths counts the ability cost, so Archer Queen plus her ability counts as 6 elixir. The wiki advises waiting until the opponent has spent elixir on an ability before answering ([CR Wiki: Archer Queen](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Archer_Queen); [CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir))
- TouchArcade's 2016 review says Clash Royale simplifies and slows RTS and MOBA style split second decisions just enough to be easy to play, while still making each decision feel as weighty. It describes double elixir as the point where both players fire cards at each other rapidly (paraphrase) ([TouchArcade review](https://toucharcade.com/2016/03/02/clash-royale-review/))
- Wikipedia describes Clash Royale as a 2016 real time strategy mobile game mixing collectible cards, tower defence and MOBA, with battles of 3 to 5 minutes ([Wikipedia: Clash Royale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clash_Royale))
- Supercell design talks found: GDC 2017, Stefan Engblom, Quest for the Healthy Metagame, on card balance principles from pre launch and the first live year ([GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024272/Quest-for-the-Healthy-Metagame)); GDC 2026, James Back and Aleksandar Markovic, Recent Bets and Outcomes from Supercell's Clash Royale, on what worked over five years, including simplifying to grow the audience ([GDC schedule](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/recent-bets-outcomes-from-supercells-clash-royale/917002)). Search snippet only: a report on an earlier talk by the same two lists novel content, easier progression, and simpler systems for returning players, accepting short term pain in metrics and sentiment ([mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/supercell-explains-clash-royales-big-comeback/))
- Search snippet only: Supercell CEO Ilkka Paananen said Clash Royale grew from an internal prototype called The Summoners made before Clash of Clans, and that real time PvP was first rejected ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/63911/clash-royale-based-on-a-pre-clans-prototype/))
- Search snippet only: a Supercell job ad for a Clash Royale UI artist names making the experience more readable as a goal ([Supercell careers](https://supercell.com/en/careers/senior-uxui-artist-clash-royale/54dd1d43-0075-496a-8948-a5f9375f5960/))
- Search snippet only: Supercell shut down its auto battler Clash Mini in 2024, saying it was a good game but not the one that would meet its goals. One report contrasts 700 thousand Clash Mini downloads in 2023 with 35.21 million for Clash Royale ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/03/15/clash-mini-shutdown-supercell-no-new-games-in-five-years))

### Inferences
- Inferred: 90.7 elixir over 3 minutes divided by a 3.5 to 4.0 average card cost is about 23 to 26 plays per match, roughly one every 7 to 8 s.
- Inferred: in 41 seconds of single elixir (14.6 elixir generated plus the 5 at the start) a Clash Royale player can make about 4 to 6 plays. That is close to the 4 orders pressed in the Kindred Siege playtest, so the raw count is probably not the main problem. The difference is that each Clash Royale play answers something visible (an enemy card just dropped), has a where as well as a when, and shows instant feedback through the trade. That points at legibility (what am I answering, did it work) more than frequency.
- Inferred: Clash Royale's escalation (x1 then x2 then x3) raises the decision rate as the match nears its end, so the busiest moments sit at the climax.
- Inferred: Champions are the nearest shipped design to Kindred Siege's chief spell: a shared, in fight resource, a short wind up (1 s) the opponent can read, then a cooldown (12 to 30 s) that stops spam. Cost runs 1 to 3 elixir, about 10 to 30% of a full bar.
- Inferred: the full bar warning (text plus shaking cards) is a cheap cue that tells the player to act. A Kindred Siege points bar with no cue when full would let points pile up unused.
- Inferred, weak: Clash Mini's failure next to Clash Royale's success fits the idea that Supercell's audience wanted real time agency, but the cited reason was quality and goals, so this is correlation only.

### Gaps
- No Supercell talk specifically about elixir rate or how often players act was found. GDC Vault recordings could not be read.
- No primary Supercell source on unit silhouettes or troop readability turned up. Claims about silhouette rules came from a low quality page and are left out.
- The support page could not be opened, so its numbers come from a search snippet only.

## 3. Bad North: real time with pause, few units, few orders

### Takeaway
Bad North cuts the RTS down to about four squads, one main order (move this squad to that tile), plus Replenish, Flee and one class skill or item per commander. Selecting a squad slows time to a crawl instead of pausing, so the player gets thinking time without losing urgency. The developers built readability through minimalism and sound, aiming for a game you could follow with your eyes shut.

### Cited Findings
- Designer Richard Meredith calls it essentially a micro RTS. The player's main action is telling a unit to go to a grid space, and there are only four or five units at most, which limits micromanagement ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=314054))
- Choosing where units go slows the game to a crawl instead of stopping it. TheSixthAxis says this keeps the urgency while giving room to fix a bad move ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=314054))
- The developers say a slow time sound effect tells the player that time has slowed so they can think, and that sound design should support readability so you could close your eyes and still follow the game (paraphrase) ([Gaming Audio News, from a Nintendo UK interview](https://gamingaudionews.com/2018/04/26/bad-norths-developers-talk-about-inspiration-behind-audio-and-gameplay/); [Nintendo UK interview](https://www.nintendo.co.uk/News/2018/April/Interview-Taking-on-hordes-of-invading-Vikings-in-Bad-North-1368315.html))
- Meredith: minimalism runs through the whole game. It targets players put off by the complexity and up front demands of a typical RTS. Many systems exist but are hidden so the game is easy to get into. The player has a small set of rules and actions, each soldier then decides for himself, and the simulation adds the complexity (paraphrase) ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/68638/indie-spotlight-plausible-concept-on-bad-north/))
- Unit behaviour stays readable: pikemen cannot attack while moving, positioning can pull enemy attention, and a small archer group can beat a large one because the large one is the easier target. Meredith says players can see this happen and see why. The reviewer found the portable mode needed lots of zooming to track enemies ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=314054))
- Up to 4 squads per island (3 of yours plus the local commander on a home island). The Vikings try to burn houses, and coins are earned for each house left standing ([Bad North Wiki: Basic Rules](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Bad_North%27s_Basic_Rules))
- Commanders are invulnerable while at least one soldier lives. If a commander dies the squad is lost for good ([Bad North Wiki: Commander](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Commander))
- Replenish: the squad enters an intact house and refills, taking 2 seconds per soldier of full squad size (16 s for 8) whatever the losses, and cannot act or cancel meanwhile. Flee: board an empty enemy boat to leave the island, and it cannot be undone ([Bad North Wiki: Regenerate and Flee](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Regenerate_and_Flee))
- Class skills: Plunge for infantry, Volley for archers, Pike Charge for pikes ([Bad North Wiki: Skills](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Skills)). Volley hits one targeted tile, has a long travel time, recharges over time, and shows a small icon over the squad when ready again ([Bad North Wiki: Volley](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Volley))
- Items use per island charges rather than cooldowns. Bomb: 1 throw per island, 2 after upgrade, with travel time, and it can hurt your own troops ([Bad North Wiki: Bomb](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Bomb)). Mines: 2 per island, rising with upgrades, set before enemies arrive ([Bad North Wiki: Mines](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Mines)). War Horn: calls soldiers out of a chosen house once per island, twice at level 3 with a 12 s cooldown ([Bad North Wiki: War Horn](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/War_Horn))

### Inferences
- Inferred: Bad North's verb count per squad is about 3 to 4 (move, one skill or item, Replenish, Flee), close to Kindred Siege's 4 orders. Its readability comes from tiny squad counts, slow time on select, distinct audio, and unit rules simple enough to see cause and effect.
- Inferred: per island charges (1 to 4 uses) make each use a visible, weighty decision without a timer to watch. Kindred Siege's points per fight could work the same way if the costs are big enough to feel.
- Inferred: Replenish and Flee are safe answers with clear costs (time locked and exposed, or giving up the island's houses), a template for Fall Back.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Bad North also has a full pause beyond slow time on selection.
- Exact recharge seconds for Volley, Plunge and Pike Charge were not found. The Bomb page lists its cooldown as missing.
- No developer statement on how many orders per minute they aimed for.

## 4. Mechabellum, Super Auto Pets, Teamfight Tactics, The Bazaar, Backpack Battles: where decisions sit and what players say about pure watching

### Takeaway
In all five, the fight itself is watched, not played, and the decisions sit before and between fights: buying, positioning, upgrading, picking cards. Their fights are short (Backpack Battles 10 to 20 s, TFT up to about 40 s, Mechabellum up to 60 s) and most have built in accelerators that end stalls. Players split: some love watching their plan play out, others say there is nothing to do once the fight starts. Pure watching designs avoid long fights with nothing to read.

### Cited Findings
**Mechabellum**
- Two phases: planning, then an automated battle. You cannot control units during the battle, but you can click enemy units to read their upgrades, so players plan the next round while watching. Pathing for a unit can be set, but only before the round starts ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/669330/discussions/0/3805027266432355508))
- Each round you unlock a unit, deploy up to 2 more and position them, and spend one shared resource on units or upgrades. The battle aims to kill all enemy units before a 60 second round timer ends, and surviving units deal damage to the opponent (GamingOnLinux, early access, 2023) ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2023/05/mechabellum-is-an-auto-battling-supreme-commander-im-completely-hooked/page=1/))
- Skills (spells) come from the Research Center or Reinforcement cards and have cooldowns scaled to their strength. Research skills include an oil bomb that slows by 55% for 2 rounds, a refund of a squad, and a Mobile Beacon that sets a unit's path ([Mechabellum Wiki: Skill](https://mechabellum.wiki.gg/wiki/Skill)). Each round 4 random reinforcement cards are offered at a cost, the same set for both players ([Mechabellum Wiki: Reinforcement](https://mechabellum.wiki.gg/wiki/Reinforcement))
- Losing a tower cripples your whole army for a short time: 80% slower, 90% less damage, 50% more damage taken, for 9 s at tower level 1 down to 3 s at level 4 ([Mechabellum Wiki: Tower](https://mechabellum.wiki.gg/wiki/Tower))
- Mike Morhaime (Dreamhaven) said it keeps the strategic depth of real time strategy without the actions per minute demand, and that you do not have to fight in real time, you can think. Replays let players study exact moments (paraphrase) ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/mechabellum-gets-a-second-chance-as-dreamhaven-relaunches-the-mech-autobattler/))
- Players on Quarter To Three: one says that although you do nothing during the battle he never tires of watching the robots fight; another says he cannot enjoy auto battlers because he has nothing to do once a battle starts; a third describes the group shouting at a big unit distracted by chaff while a threat wrecked the army (paraphrase) ([Quarter To Three thread](https://forum.quartertothree.com/t/mechabellum-tactical-autobattler-against-other-people/167835))
- Search snippet only: in early access some players said late round deployment time felt too short, with some finishing moves in about 20 seconds ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/669330/discussions/0/3805026824438136946/?ctp=2))

**Super Auto Pets**
- Each turn has a preparation phase (buy pets and food, level pets, reorder them, 10 gold per turn) and then a battle where the pets fight automatically with no player control. The front pet fights the enemy's front pet until a team runs out. In Arena mode players can take as long as they like to prepare ([Wikipedia: Super Auto Pets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Auto_Pets))
- Steam calls Arena mode relaxed asynchronous play without timers and Versus mode an intense synchronous 8 player game with quick decisions. Reviews: 91% positive of 25,092 ([Steam: Super Auto Pets](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1714040/))

**Teamfight Tactics**
- Each round starts with a 30 second planning phase for positioning. In PvP rounds teams fight until one is wiped or 40 s pass. The wiki also lists an overtime that starts 30 s into a battle and triples attack speed for 15 s ([LoL Wiki: TFT](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Teamfight_Tactics_(game)))
- During combat you can still buy champions, but they do not join the current fight. You can put items on fighting champions and the effects apply from then on, except start of combat effects ([LoL Wiki: TFT](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Teamfight_Tactics_(game)))
- Riot's launch text: champions move, attack and cast spells automatically, and success comes from team composition and positioning ([LoL Wiki: TFT](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Teamfight_Tactics_(game)))
- A French guide says you have little control during the fight because most of the work happens before, and that abilities fire on their own when mana fills ([Millenium TFT guide](https://www.millenium.org/guide/336393.html))

**The Bazaar**
- A free to play hero builder by Tempo, the studio founded and run by Reynad, released March 2025 ([The Bazaar Wiki](https://thebazaar.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Bazaar)). PvP is asynchronous and fully automated (search snippet) ([Cardgamer](https://cardgamer.com/games/digital-card-games/the-bazaar/6-reasons-why-the-bazaars-open-beta-caused-an-uproar))
- Items fire on their own cooldowns. The wiki search snippets I saw ran about 5 to 10 s per item (for example Bottled Tornado at 7 s), so treat that range as a sample, not a rule ([The Bazaar Wiki: Bottled Tornado](https://thebazaar.wiki.gg/wiki/Bottled_Tornado))
- Patch 0.1.3 goals included slowing the pace of endgame combat, and the patch sharply raised the Sandstorm's minimum tick rate to speed up the end of fights ([The Bazaar Wiki: Version 0.1.3](https://thebazaar.wiki.gg/wiki/Version_0.1.3))
- Search snippet only: a Steam commenter said The Bazaar has too much between battles, leaning on clicking and waiting through animations ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2427700/discussions/0/4626978969929912552/?ctp=4))

**Backpack Battles**
- Up to 18 rounds of shop phase then battle phase. In battle your character fights another player's character asynchronously with no player control. Items trigger on their own timers. Battles typically last about 10 to 20 seconds ([Wikipedia: Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles))
- Fatigue stops endless fights: at 17 seconds (nightfall) both sides take damage every second that rises each tick, and grows faster after 60 seconds ([Backpack Battles Wiki: Fatigue](https://backpackbattles.wiki.gg/wiki/Fatigue))

**Wider complaints about pure watching**
- Gratuitous Space Battles (search snippets only, pages not opened): GamesRadar listed being unable to control the battles as a con ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/gratuitous-space-battles-review)). The developer, Cliff Harris, compared the player to generals of old who set formations and then had to watch, and said ship commanders have full autonomy once the battle starts ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/58533/who-likes-gratuitous-space-battles)). A Giant Bomb user review said seeing a setup carve up a fleet makes it hard to resist ([Giant Bomb user review](https://giantbomb.com/user-reviews/id/59551/gratuitous-in-every-way))
- An itch.io commenter on a jam auto battler called the player's lack of agency over the gameplay one of its biggest problems, and suggested letting the player queue card picks by hand ([itch.io comment](https://itch.io/post/13487129))
- Search snippet only: Hearthstone Battlegrounds players have complained about long combat animations stretching the combat phase and asked for an optional speed up ([Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/battlegrounds-animation-issues/135955))

### Inferences
- Inferred: these games make watching bearable by keeping fights short (10 to 40 s, Mechabellum up to 60 s), forcing endings (Fatigue at 17 s, TFT overtime at 30 s, Sandstorm), and giving the watcher something to read (inspect enemy units in Mechabellum, items on visible timers in The Bazaar and Backpack Battles).
- Inferred: a 41 second Kindred Siege fight with sparse orders sits in an awkward middle: longer than most pure watch fights, but without the steady input of Kingdom Rush or Clash Royale. Either the fight gets shorter, or there must be something to answer every few seconds.
- Inferred: TFT's buy and equip during combat is a small in fight verb that does not change the fight's rules. It keeps hands busy and planning ahead while the fight resolves.

### Gaps
- No exact Mechabellum deployment timer length, and the 60 s round timer comes from an early access review that may be out of date. How Mechabellum skills like Orbital Bombardment are timed during battle was not confirmed.
- When the Sandstorm starts in The Bazaar was not found.
- No developer statement from Super Auto Pets or Backpack Battles on why fights have no control.

## 5. Warpack, Northgard Battlegrounds, Time Walker: Dark World: does the commander step into the fight?

### Takeaway
None of the three, as described in their own material, lets the player act during the fight. Their commander or warchief layer sits between fights: commander skills picked as you win (Warpack), settlement layout, blessings and Fate cards (Northgard Battlegrounds), and formation, gear and skills set before each room (Time Walker). Two of them are not fully released as of 25 October 2026, so this can change.

### Cited Findings
- Warpack (N1ghtmare Studios): an asynchronous PvP auto battler where every opponent is another player's saved army. Each unit has a 3x3 backpack with shaped items and adjacency combos, units level by eating other units and absorbing their traits, commander skills are picked as you win rounds, and a ranked run takes about 10 minutes. Release listed for 10 November 2026, with a demo out. The page does not say whether commander skills are used during a fight ([Steam: Warpack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5016130/); [Nodal](https://nodal.gg/game/warpack-5016130))
- Northgard Battlegrounds: developed by Funday Games (Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor), published by Shiro Unlimited. A city builder and auto battler with asynchronous PvP. The official text says the warbands clash in automatic battles, and that the player commands the strategy while the warriors fight (paraphrase). Building placement, blessings and earlier choices decide how the warband performs ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/northgard-battlegrounds-announced-for-pc))
- Northgard Battlegrounds open beta ran 9 to 15 July 2026 with the Raven and Stag clans and their own skills, over 50 structures and three patrons ([Rogueliker](https://rogueliker.com/northgard-battlegrounds-open-beta/)). Search snippets: a 19 October 2026 demo update added the Clan of the Wolf and over 100 Fate cards ([Gamespress](https://www.gamespress.com/Shiro-Unlimited-Storms-Steam-Next-Fest-With-Frostrail-Demo-and-Northga)), and retailer and news listings give Early Access as 27 October 2026 ([Gamesplanet](https://us.gamesplanet.com/community/northgard-battlegrounds-steam-key--8406-1/news_updates/431961-city-building-clan-based-brawler-northgard); [GameWatcher](https://www.gamewatcher.com/northgard-battlegrounds/guides/release-date-confirmed-2026-platforms-pre-orders-trailers))
- Time Walker: Dark World: a roguelike auto battler where you lead four heroes, with over 80 skills on Steam (Nintendo listings say over 40) ([Steam: Time Walker](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2093910/)). Search snippets: developed by Heartfun, published by Leyo Games and 2P Games, PC release 29 November 2023 ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/time-walker-dark-world/info))
- Automaton (Japanese press) says Time Walker's combat is automatic and the player does not dodge or time counterattacks. - Inferred: the newest commander themed auto battlers keep the commander out of the fight and give him a between fights build layer. Kindred Siege's live orders during the fight make it closer to Kingdom Rush or Clash Royale than to these games, so they are weak models for in fight pacing, but useful for how they reward builds.
- Inferred: Time Walker's 60 second room limit with zero in fight input is an example of a long fight people accept, likely because the player's job (build and formation) is done before the clock starts.

### Gaps

## 6. Squads that fight on their own with a small set of timed abilities; how many actions per minute engage versus overwhelm

### Takeaway
The shipped pattern is: units fight by themselves, the player holds 1 to 5 ability buttons (Kingdom Rush spells and heroes, AFK Journey ultimates, Clash Royale champion abilities, Clash of Clans hero abilities), and abilities are gated by cooldown, a filling energy bar, an elixir cost, or a once per battle charge. Games that ask for aim slow time while you aim. For action rate, the best hard data is RTS research: even bronze league StarCraft 2 players complete a look then act cycle about every 5 seconds. No study gives an ideal rate for casual auto battlers.

### Cited Findings
- AFK Journey: the player sets positions before the fight, then heroes act on their own. Abilities can be triggered by hand or set to automatic, and triggering by hand slows the battle so the player can aim, for example waiting for enemies to walk into an area effect. Explosive barrels can be set off by hand and can hurt your own heroes ([Pocket Gamer AFK Journey guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/afk-journey/combat-guide))
- AFK Journey, search snippets only: the ultimate is the manual ability and fires when a yellow energy bar is full, haste speeds up charging, some heroes generate energy for the team, and there is full auto plus a 3x speed option ([BlueStacks guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/afk-2-journey/afkj-beginners-guide-en.html))
- Clash of Clans: a hero's ability can be used once per battle (for example the Barbarian King's heal) ([CoC Wiki: Barbarian King](https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Barbarian_King))
- Clash Royale champions pair a cost (1 to 3 elixir) with a cooldown (12 to 30 s) and a 1 s wind up (see section 2) ([CR Wiki: Archer Queen](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Archer_Queen))
- Kingdom Rush heroes use their normal skills on their own; the player moves them and fires one ultimate each (see section 1) ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush: Vengeance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush:_Vengeance))
- FTL (pausable, crew act on their own): the wiki stresses that weapons work far better fired as a well timed volley, because badly timed single shots are wasted on shields that regenerate before the next shot lands ([FTL Wiki: Weapons](https://ftl.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons))
- Pillars of Eternity II (real time with pause) added a turn based mode. Josh Sawyer said not many players stop to read the armour ratings on enemies while combat runs in real time, and that turn based play allows much more deliberation. He framed it as a pace for players who enjoy slower combat (paraphrase) ([GameBanshee](http://www.gamebanshee.com/k4fww))
- SkillCraft (Thompson and others, PLOS ONE 2013, over 3,000 StarCraft 2 players from bronze to professional): mean APM was 117 (standard deviation 52). Play breaks into perception action cycles (look at a spot, act, move on) covering about 87% of game time, with about 5.3 actions per cycle and 18.4 cycles per minute on average. Bronze players take about five seconds per cycle and professionals about half that. Mean first action latency was about 720 ms and was among the strongest predictors of skill ([PLOS ONE: SkillCraft](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0075129))
- Mike Morhaime pitched Mechabellum as RTS depth without the APM demand (see section 4) ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/mechabellum-gets-a-second-chance-as-dreamhaven-relaunches-the-mech-autobattler/))

### Inferences
- Inferred: one perception action cycle every 5 s (bronze) is about 8 in a 41 second fight; pros manage about 16. The Kindred Siege playtest had 4 presses, about one every 10 s. Kingdom Rush and Clash Royale sit near one input every 5 to 10 s. So a casual target of one meaningful look and act every 5 to 10 seconds fits the shipped games; more than one every 2 to 3 s is pro RTS territory. This is a range from analogy, not a measured comfort threshold.
- Inferred: the number of buttons is not unusual. Clash Royale shows 4 cards, Kingdom Rush Alliance 3 spells plus 2 heroes, Bad North about 3 to 4 verbs per squad. Kindred Siege's 4 orders plus 1 spell is in range; the problem is more likely what tells the player when and why to press each one.
- Inferred: an energy bar that fills during the fight (AFK Journey) is the nearest match to Kindred Siege's points. AFK Journey offers auto cast and slows time on manual aim, two separate ways to lower the load.
- Inferred: Sawyer's point applies directly: in real time, players do not read detailed stats. Whatever Hold, Fall Back, Focus Fire and Unleash answer has to show up as a big, simple signal, not as numbers.

### Gaps
- No Warcraft III hero cooldown figures were gathered (the wiki pages found were for World of Warcraft).
- No study of comfortable APM for casual or mobile players; SkillCraft covers ranked RTS players only.
- Darkest Dungeon's decision density (turn based) was not researched.
- AFK Journey's energy numbers come from secondary guides only.

## 7. Design principles that recur

### Takeaway
Six principles show up across the sources: (1) keep the player making consequential choices tied to what they care about right now; (2) mix verb tiers, cheap and frequent next to rare and big; (3) gate with a shared resource when you want trade offs between verbs, and with cooldowns when you want each verb to have its own rhythm, often both; (4) telegraph threats so the answer is a readable choice and failure feels fair; (5) when you ask for aim or timing, slow the world or give a short wind up; (6) pay the risky play more than the safe one, and give the safe play a visible cost.

### Cited Findings
- Engagement: Steven Mathers (Game Developer blog, 2014) argues players stay engaged only while they can make consequential decisions toward the goals they currently care about, that micromanagement turns into a chore when it does not serve those goals, and that players only hand work to automation that is reliable (paraphrase) ([Game Developer: Rules of Engagement](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/rules-of-engagement))
- Decision layering: Adam Wentworth's 2017 Clemson MFA thesis frames pacing as a Player Decision Cycle: several types of decisions overlap, each recurring many times at its own rate, and their interplay sets the pace ([Clemson thesis](https://open.clemson.edu/all_theses/2764))
- Busy by design: Ironhide's stated aim was a player who is involved and busy rather than one who plans and then watches ([Kodeco interview](https://www.kodeco.com/2769-making-a-hit-tower-defense-game-a-top-app-dev-interview))
- Cooldown only, free to cast: Kingdom Rush spells cost nothing but time. The short Reinforcements cooldown makes it something to spam, and the long cooldowns of the big spells make them crisis tools ([KR Wiki: Call Reinforcements](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Call_Reinforcements); [KR Wiki: Rain of Fire](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_of_Fire))
- Shared resource: Clash Royale puts every action on one elixir bar, so each play competes with every other and trades can be scored ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir)). Champion abilities add a cooldown on top of the elixir cost ([CR Wiki: Archer Queen](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Archer_Queen))
- Charges per encounter: Bad North items give 1 to 4 uses per island ([Bad North Wiki: Bomb](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Bomb); [Bad North Wiki: Mines](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Mines)); Clash of Clans heroes get one ability per battle ([CoC Wiki: Barbarian King](https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Barbarian_King))
- Energy earned in the fight: AFK Journey ultimates wait on an energy bar ([BlueStacks guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/afk-2-journey/afkj-beginners-guide-en.html))
- Rewarding use: Kingdom Rush 6 gives each spell XP on every cast ([Wikily: KR6 spells](https://wikily.gg/kingdom-rush-6-genesis/spells)); Kingdom Rush lowers spell cooldowns when you call a wave early ([Ayumilove KR guide](https://ayumilove.net/kingdom-rush-walkthrough-guide/))
- Aimed versus not aimed: Rain of Fire is aimed with fall time, so guides say aim ahead ([Ayumilove KR guide](https://ayumilove.net/kingdom-rush-walkthrough-guide/)); Thunderbolt hits at once and is called more accurate for it ([KR Wiki: Thunderbolt](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Thunderbolt)); Soul Impact must be dropped right on the enemy ([KR Wiki: Soul Impact](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Impact)); Bad North's Bomb and Volley have travel time that has to be planned for ([Bad North Wiki: Bomb](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Bomb); [Bad North Wiki: Volley](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Volley)); Clash Royale champion abilities are not aimed at all, only timed ([CR Wiki: Golden Knight](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Knight))
- Time help for aiming and timing: Bad North slows time while you choose ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=314054)); AFK Journey slows time while you aim a manual ability ([Pocket Gamer AFK Journey guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/afk-journey/combat-guide)); Kingdom Rush has a pause key ([Ayumilove KR guide](https://ayumilove.net/kingdom-rush-walkthrough-guide/))
- Telegraph then answer: Subset Games' Justin Ma said they wanted every death in Into the Breach to feel like the player's own fault, which led to telegraphed enemy attacks as a core mechanic, and that it should be clear how and why you failed so you can improve (paraphrase) ([Game Developer: Road to the IGF](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-subset-games-i-into-the-breach-i-)). Clash Royale's 1 s champion wind up and visible card drops give both players something to read and answer ([CR Wiki: Monk](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Monk)). Kingdom Rush Alliance lets you reopen enemy intro cards mid stage ([KR Wiki: Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Rush_5:_Alliance))
- Risk versus safe: in Clash Royale the defender usually wins trades, overcommitting hands the opponent a counter, and the reactive Monk reflect is high risk and high reward with a tiny window ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir); [CR Wiki: Monk](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Monk)). Bad North's Flee is the safe exit that gives up the island and cannot be undone, and Replenish locks the squad for 2 s per soldier ([Bad North Wiki: Regenerate and Flee](https://bad-north.fandom.com/wiki/Regenerate_and_Flee)). Kingdom Rush's early wave call trades safety for gold and faster spells ([Ayumilove KR guide](https://ayumilove.net/kingdom-rush-walkthrough-guide/))
- Escalation: Clash Royale's x1, x2, x3 elixir ([CR Wiki: Elixir](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir)); TFT overtime ([LoL Wiki: TFT](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Teamfight_Tactics_(game))); Backpack Battles Fatigue ([Backpack Battles Wiki: Fatigue](https://backpackbattles.wiki.gg/wiki/Fatigue)); The Bazaar Sandstorm ([The Bazaar Wiki: Version 0.1.3](https://thebazaar.wiki.gg/wiki/Version_0.1.3))
- Readability through sound and few moving parts: Bad North ([Gaming Audio News](https://gamingaudionews.com/2018/04/26/bad-norths-developers-talk-about-inspiration-behind-audio-and-gameplay/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/68638/indie-spotlight-plausible-concept-on-bad-north/)); hand made waves keep surprise without randomness in Kingdom Rush ([ENTER.CO interview](https://www.enter.co/especiales/videojuegos/la-ciencia-y-el-arte-detras-de-un-buen-tower-defense-entrevista/))

### Inferences
- Inferred, applied to Kindred Siege: give each order a clear trigger the player can see. Hold as a parry needs a readable enemy wind up (the Into the Breach and Clash Royale lesson). Fall Back needs a visible threat worth leaving. Focus Fire needs an obvious priority target. Unleash needs a moment where a burst plainly pays off.
- Inferred: make one order cheap and nearly always useful (the Reinforcements role) so the player builds the habit of pressing, and keep one rare and big (the Rain of Fire role) for the climax.
- Inferred: points earned in the fight work best with a full bar cue and a reason not to hoard (Clash Royale's leak warning, Kingdom Rush 6's XP per cast).
- Inferred: since the player said he did not know what was going on, slow time on order selection (Bad North, AFK Journey) and strong audio for each enemy tell are proven fixes worth testing before adding more verbs.
- Inferred: the safe answer (Fall Back) should cost something visible, like Bad North's Flee and Replenish, and the timed Hold parry should pay more, in line with Clash Royale's high risk reactive plays.

### Gaps
- No source gives one agreed ideal decision rate; the 5 to 10 second figure above is my synthesis.
- No direct study compares cooldown gating with resource gating on player engagement; the comparison here comes from shipped designs.
- Into the Breach is turn based. Whether its telegraph rule holds as well at real time speeds is not tested by any source here.

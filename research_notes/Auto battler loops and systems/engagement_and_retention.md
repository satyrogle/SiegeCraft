# Engagement and retention in auto battlers: rewards, decisions, session design, meta progression and market evidence

Notes for Kindred Siege. Gathered 12 Oct 2026. Player figures are SteamCharts monthly averages and peaks read on 12 Oct 2026 unless stated. Steam leaves out mobile, browser and own launchers, so it undercounts Super Auto Pets and The Bazaar and says nothing about TFT or Hearthstone. Labels: [snippet] means the claim came from a search result summary because the page did not load or was not opened in full; [community] means players or fan sites; [opinion] means a writer's or developer's view rather than data; [peer reviewed] marks journal or conference research. The session's web search budget ran out near the end, so a few planned checks sit under Gaps.

## 1. Reward structures: variable rewards, high rolls, build discovery, near misses and comeback mechanics

### Takeaway
Auto battler designers openly use randomness as the engine of replay, but the long lived games learned to keep it positive, chosen and readable: rare chase outcomes, pick one of three offers, a top half finish counted as a win, and payouts for losing streaks. Random punishment the player cannot answer drove players off, and near miss and suspense effects are real in lab research but untested in auto battlers.

### Cited Findings
**Riot on randomness and high rolls (Teamfight Tactics)**
- Riot's review of TFT's first set (Riot_Mort, 28 Oct 2019) says randomness drives the game: players play around variance from the shop, item drops and in game effects; that variance gives variety and replayability, and skill shows in adapting to the possible outcomes. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Same post: a two unit Imperial bonus was effectively a coin flip on double damage for your main carry, and players accepted it because they opted into that risk and could predict the outcomes. Phantom and Hextech, which could set a unit to 100 HP or take away all three of its items with no possible response, drew a strong negative reaction. Phantom made exciting comebacks but cost the overall experience too much. Riot's stated goal for Set 2 was to keep random effects in positive, controlled spaces, and Thieves Gloves moved from any two random items to a curated list. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Same post: very unlikely chase outcomes, such as fielding nine Blademasters or nine Sorcerers, created memorable moments, and Riot said it would keep adding them. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Same post: 4th place or better should always feel like a win, so from Set 2 a top four finish never cost ranked points. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Riot's Reckoning review (8 Oct 2021): the Fortune economy trait was very popular. Draconic fell short because hoarding champions toward three star beat selling them, and its golden eggs on turns 4 and 5 were too rare. Players wanted daring, high payoff moments, so Riot said it would focus on accessible, exciting economy traits with more dice, treasure and comebacks. ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/))
- Same post: Shadow items could be skipped entirely, which Riot read as a bad sign for a core mechanic. The replacements (Radiant items, Radiant Blessings, Tome of Traits) were easier to enjoy and produced more high moments. ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/))
- Riot's Gizmos & Gadgets review (2 May 2022): players asked for new experiences such as hitting a three star Galio or combining the Level Up and High End Shopping augments, and Riot made variance and novelty its top development priority. One late PvE round often dropped item components that did not fit the player's board, leaving them with a dead game, and Riot promised a major change. Riot was also considering systems to stop worst case augment outcomes. ([Riot, Gizmos & Gadgets learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-gizmos-gadgets-learnings/))
- Mort (Riot), May 2022: augments added a lot of variance from game to game, which he called important for TFT's long term health and the reason it is fun to come back to. Novelty mattered more to players than nostalgia, and the excitement over reprinted traits faded fast. ([RiftFeed, 25 May 2022](https://riftfeed.gg/more/tft-set-7-dargonlands-mort-interview))

**Economy and comeback payouts**
- Player guides describe TFT interest as 1 gold per 10 banked, up to 5 gold at 50, plus bonus gold for both win streaks and loss streaks. The streak table differs between sources and patches, and no current official Riot table was found. [community] ([Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/how-to-manage-your-economy-in-teamfight-tactics-three-strategies/); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/085670/how-to-econ-in-tft); [Upcomer, marked outdated](https://upcomer.com/?p=55458))
- A player thread claims Riot keeps a loss streak trait in almost every set because players engage with it heavily. Unconfirmed player claim; only a mirror of the subreddit listing surfaced. [community] [snippet] ([r/CompetitiveTFT mirror](https://reddit.sudovanilla.org/r/CompetitiveTFT))
- Hearthstone Battlegrounds: a 1st to 4th finish counts as a win for rating. The loser of each fight takes damage equal to the winner's tavern tier plus the tiers of its surviving minions. A rule added 21 Jan 2020 stops you meeting the same opponent more than once in any three rounds. ([Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds))
- Super Auto Pets Arena: a win gives a trophy, a loss costs a life, a draw changes nothing, and if you lost a life in the previous two turns you get one back on turn 3. Ten trophies wins the run. ([Wikipedia, Super Auto Pets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Auto_Pets))
- The Bazaar pays chests at win milestones: 4 wins give 2 chests, 7 wins give 4, 10 wins give 6 in one guide, with ranked paying more at 10 according to another. [snippet] ([Mobalytics day guide](https://mobalytics.gg/the-bazaar/guides/day-guide))

**Build discovery**
- Backpack Battles: rarer items appear more often in later rounds, and items merge through adjacency recipes after fights; for example a Hero Sword next to two Whetstones becomes a Hero Longsword. ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles))
- Battlegrounds lead designer Mitchell Loewen (19 Aug 2024) said some trinkets have special rules deliberately left for players to discover. ([Hearthstone Top Decks](https://www.hearthstonetopdecks.com/developer-insights-battlegrounds-trinkets/))
- The Bazaar has merchants that sell powerful items and events with unexpected outcomes. ([Game8](https://game8.co/articles/games/the-bazaar))
- Hearthstone designer Dean Ayala shared in Mar 2020 that Reno Jackson was the 4th most picked hero but 24th by average placement, and that Lord Jaraxxus placed well under 5000 rating but 28th above it. ([Inven Global, 9 Mar 2020](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10616/hearthstone-developer-dean-ayala-shares-10-fun-stats-about-battlegrounds))

**How writers describe the pull**
- A Dec 2019 analysis credited Dota Auto Chess's rise to its mix of strategy and a slot machine style unit shop, plus constant updates. [opinion] ([Inven Global, 29 Dec 2019](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10060/2019-started-as-the-year-of-the-autobattler-but-did-it-end-that-way-too))
- A 2024 essay argues TFT's blend of random elements and strategy raises the likelihood of addiction, comparing it to slot machines. It has no data. [opinion] [snippet] ([Mechanics of Magic](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2024/05/31/strategic-depth-and-mobile-mastery-a-reflection-on-teamfight-tactics))

**Lab research on close outcomes** [peer reviewed]
- Clark and colleagues (Neuron, 2009, 61(3)) used a simplified slot machine. Near misses felt less pleasant than full misses yet raised the desire to play, and they activated reward areas that wins also activated. The motivating effect appeared only when the player had personal control over arranging the gamble. The fMRI sample was small (15 people per one account) and none were regular or problem gamblers. ([PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2658737/); [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2009/02/090211122130.htm)) [snippet]
- Abuhamdeh, Csikszentmihalyi and Jalal (Motivation and Emotion, 2015, 39(1)): feeling competent and feeling suspense each raised enjoyment, fairly independently. Enjoyment peaked when players were slightly ahead. Offered one more game, 69% chose the game they had narrowly won over the one they won easily. Study 1 had 72 students. ([BPS Research Digest, 23 Jan 2015](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/why-risk-losing-more-fun-easy-win))

### Inferences
- Riot's own reviews show one stable rule across three years: random upside the player opted into is kept, random downside with no answer is cut. This is one studio's practitioner evidence, not research.
- Rewards come in layers in every game here: each shop and fight, a run result that counts the top half as a win or pays chests at milestones, and rare chase outcomes. Several games also soften a losing run (loss streak gold, Super Auto Pets' life back on turn 3, Battlegrounds damage that is small early), which keeps a bad run worth playing.
- Reno Jackson's pick rate far above his results suggests players pick for spectacle as well as strength. The post gives no reason, so this is a guess.
- For Kindred Siege (implication, not a design call): the owner's "no reward" matches the absence of these layers. A close fight in which the player gave orders sits near the conditions in the near miss and suspense studies (personal control, close outcome), but those studies used a slot machine and a sword game, so the transfer is unproven. Deliberately rigging close losses would move into manipulation (section 7).

### Gaps
- No designer statement on near misses in auto battlers was found.
- Fortune's exact rule (as I recall, a loss streak that pays out when you next win) was not checked in this pass.
- No official current TFT economy table was found.

## 2. Risk and reward decisions: save or spend, roll or level, greed, pivots, selling

### Takeaway
The tension comes from a few repeated trades between two things the player can read: bank gold for interest or spend now, level or roll, hold units for an upgrade or sell, commit or pivot, and how much health to gamble for economy. Riot's data shows that removing the banking decision made TFT more stressful, not calmer, and that hard counters killed builds while soft counters kept them alive.

### Cited Findings
- TFT guides frame the main trade as saving versus spending. A common rule is to reach 50 gold early and spend only the excess. Selling a unit to hit 50 earns 5 interest instead of 4 at 49. Aggressive play builds a win streak that refunds spending, while loss streaking too long can kill you with 50 gold still banked. [community] ([Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/how-to-manage-your-economy-in-teamfight-tactics-three-strategies/); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/085670/how-to-econ-in-tft))
- Riot fenced the timing of economy augments: in Set 7 Rich Get Richer could only appear at stage 2 round 1 and the stronger version only at stage 3 round 2, so they could be balanced. ([RiftFeed](https://riftfeed.gg/more/tft-set-7-dargonlands-mort-interview))
- Draconic failed partly because hoarding champions toward three star was more appealing than selling them. ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/))
- Hyper Roll removed interest and started players on 20 health instead of 100, so there was no reason to bank and players rolled freely. [snippet] ([Looper](https://www.looper.com/380586/teamfight-tactics-labs-and-hyper-roll-mode-explained); [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/teamfight-tactics/everything-you-need-to-know-about-tft-hyper-roll)) Design lead Ananda Gupta's stated aim was to shorten the game a lot without cutting any phase; Riot also meant it to let new players learn without mastering economy. [snippet] ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/?p=266042))
- Riot's verdict on Hyper Roll: it averaged about 20 minutes but did not become easier to learn, and with no downtime the early and middle game felt chaotic. ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/)) Constant decisions could make it more stressful than normal TFT. ([Riot, Gizmos & Gadgets learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-gizmos-gadgets-learnings/)) In May 2025 game director Peter Whalen said it made up around 2% of total TFT playtime, had not met Riot's engagement bar and was stressful for newcomers; Riot spun it down and patch 14.8 was its last as a permanent mode. [snippet] ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/teamfight-tactics/tft-roadmap-2025-no-more-hyper-roll-pengus-party-returns-and-new-set-revival); [Riot dev update](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/dev/dev-hyper-roll-and-game-modes-update/); [Riot patch 14.8 notes](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-14-8-notes-2025/))
- Hearthstone Battlegrounds removes banking. Coins start at 3, rise by 1 a round to 10 and do not carry over. Minions cost 3, a refresh 1, and selling gives 1 back. The tavern upgrade gets 1 coin cheaper each round you wait, so the trade is tier up now or buy power now. Freezing holds a shop. ([Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds))
- Super Auto Pets gives 10 gold each turn for pets, food and rolls; selling and some pet abilities add more. ([Wikipedia, Super Auto Pets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Auto_Pets))
- Backpack Battles turns space into a resource: you buy bags (capacity) as well as items, sell, and reroll, and placement decides what triggers in the automatic fight. ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles); [GamesFuze](https://gamesfuze.com/guides/backpack-battles-ultimate-beginners-guide-tips-and-tricks/)) [snippet]
- The Bazaar scales the stake by day. You start with 20 prestige and a PvP loss costs prestige equal to the current day, so early losses are cheap and late ones expensive. The goal is 10 wins. [snippet] ([Mobalytics prestige guide](https://mobalytics.gg/the-bazaar/guides/prestige))
- Mechabellum: each round's supply buys units or temporary and permanent upgrades; once placed, units cannot be moved or controlled. ([Wikipedia, Mechabellum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechabellum))
- Counters: in TFT's first set, hard counters (100% magic immunity, 100% heal block) made the countered builds unviable. Cutting Grievous Wounds to 80% heal reduction kept it strong while healing builds stayed viable, and Set 2 traits moved to soft counters. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Control: Riot found disables feel bad because they take away control; predictable ones that could be played around were acceptable. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Too much randomness: PC Gamer's June 2021 review of Storybook Brawl called it crowded and said the sheer number of random elements made games vary wildly. ([Wikipedia, Storybook Brawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storybook_Brawl)) Commentary on Underlords named too much randomness (bad hero offers meaning you cannot win) and added complexity as possible reasons for its decline; which outlet said which was not confirmed because the PC Gamer article body did not load. [snippet] [opinion] ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/what-happened-to-autobattlers/); [UK Gaming Computers](https://ukgamingcomputers.co.uk/blog/what-was-the-auto-chess-hype-all-about-anyway))

### Inferences
- Banking and streak systems do two jobs: they create the greed choice and they give the player calm turns. The Hyper Roll result suggests a mode of constant forced spending feels harder, not easier.
- Every decision that lasted in these games is a trade between two readable goods: gold now or later, tier or board, item or space, early prestige or late prestige. The owner's "no risk" would need trades of that shape; which currency carries them has to come from his locked design.
- Riot's soft counter lesson bears directly on tribes that learn the player's habits: learning that hard counters a habit makes it unviable, while learning that only taxes it keeps it playable at a cost. This is an analogy from PvP balance and is untested in a single player game.

### Gaps
- No public data on how often players bank, roll, pivot or sell.
- No Tempo statement explaining The Bazaar's shop design was found.

## 3. Run variety: augments, portals, heroes, anomalies, packs, merchants, classes

### Takeaway
Every long lived auto battler stacks a per game rule changer on top of its unit pool: TFT augments (Nov 2021) and portals (2023), Battlegrounds heroes plus anomalies, quests, buddies and trinkets, Super Auto Pets packs and a weekly pack, The Bazaar's heroes and day events, Backpack Battles classes and subclasses. Riot calls variety and novelty its top priority, reprints went stale, and a thin item pool was the main early complaint about Backpack Battles.

### Cited Findings
**Teamfight Tactics**
- Set 6 (Gizmos & Gadgets, Nov 2021) added Hextech Augments: pick one of three at three points in a game, about 140 at launch, built to bend or break basic rules so comps that were not otherwise possible could appear. The exact stages changed during testing. [snippet] ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/teamfighttactics/articles/15094/teamfight-tactics-set-six-reveal-release-window-new-mechanic); [Upcomer](https://upcomer.com/tft-set-6-augments-will-make-every-game-fun-and-fresh/))
- Riot's review: augments added depth and variance without the layered complexity of Shadow and Radiant items, so they stayed. The risks named were bloat for new or returning players and launch power that came in too hot, and Riot was reviewing whether three per game and their timing were right. ([Riot, Gizmos & Gadgets learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-gizmos-gadgets-learnings/))
- The trade off Riot named a set earlier: the Armory gave choice and agency but reduced variance from game to game compared with the Chosen mechanic, and Riot planned to add agency while keeping variance. ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/))
- In 2026 Riot's head of gameplay Alex Cole called augments a turning point that defined TFT's identity. [snippet] ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/teamfight-tactics-turns-7-riot-games-discusses-lessons-learned-and-the-road-ahead-exclusive-interview/)) A 2026 Sheep Esports interview is headlined with the developers saying TFT has essentially reached its limit of complexity but will always reinvent itself, with the 18th set about to land. [snippet, headline and intro only] ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/we-ve-essentially-reached-the-limit-of-complexity-but-tft-will-always-re-invent-itself-game-devs/en))
- Portals (Set 9, 2023): at the start each player votes among three region portals; one portal with at least one vote is picked at random and sets a special rule for the whole lobby, for example no augments that game, or PvE rounds replaced by crabs that drop extra loot. [snippet] ([Tracker.gg](https://tracker.gg/tft/articles/all-portals-in-teamfight-tactics-tft-set-9); [Altchar](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/teamfight-tactics-set-9-runeterra-reforged-region-portals-explained-abNWC5j1hl3V)) Coverage reports Mortdog calling portals one of TFT's greatest hits. [snippet] ([AFK Gaming](https://afkgaming.com/esports/guide/will-tft-set-10-have-region-portals))
- Cadence: in Nov 2023 Mortdog said auto battlers typically stay fun for only two to four months, so TFT ships three sets a year with modes in between and patches more often than rivals. He said Set 2 (Oct 2019) nearly ended TFT: it shipped while many players still enjoyed Set 1, it pushed out players who did not want frequent change, and it had weak design choices; the team was ready to shut TFT if Set 3 failed. ([MeinMMO, 25 Nov 2023](https://mein-mmo.de/chef-tft-spiel-kurz-vor-tod-heute-besser-als-je-zuvor/))
- Riot also found change could come too fast. In Set 1 most patches carried a major expansion, and big drops excited players more than small ones, so Set 2 moved to 3 or 4 larger content patches. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/)) Balance swings that dropped comps from top tier to bottom wasted players' time. ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/))
- Tocker's Trials, TFT's single player PvE mode (27 Aug to 24 Sep 2024): 30 rounds, six bosses, three lives, no timer, and a Chaos mode for extra difficulty. Riot called a PvE mode brand new for TFT and experimental. ([Inven Global, 13 Aug 2024](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18925/when-does-pve-mode-tockers-trials-come-to-teamfight-tactics)) It came back for Set 14 ([u.gg](https://u.gg/tft/tockers-trials/comps)) and does not award seasonal pass points ([Riot support](https://support-teamfighttactics.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/32811697706387-TeamFight-Tactics-Tocker-s-Trials)). [snippet]

**Hearthstone Battlegrounds**
- 123 heroes in the current wiki count. Each player is offered 2, or 4 with the paid Season Pass, and no hero is offered to two players in one lobby. Seasons since Season 1 (Naga) have added buddies, quests, anomalies, trinkets, spells and Duos (Duos announced 4 Nov 2023). ([Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds))
- One anomaly applies to the whole lobby and is shown to everyone before hero choice; a later pool removed anomalies players disliked. [snippet] ([BlizzardWatch](https://blizzardwatch.com/2023/09/12/anomalies-hearthstone-battlegrounds-season-5/))
- Trinkets (Season 8, launched 20 Aug 2024): Loewen's stated goals were to stay approachable for new players while rewarding engaged ones, and to work without homework. Each offer always has at least one typed and one untyped trinket and at least one costing 2 or less, and off type trinkets cost 1 less. ([Hearthstone Top Decks](https://www.hearthstonetopdecks.com/developer-insights-battlegrounds-trinkets/))

**Super Auto Pets**
- Seven packs are available at any time: Turtle (60 pets, 16 foods, the original), Puppy (50 more pets, reworked June 2023 around toys), Star (83 more), Golden (74 more), Unicorn (96 more, the largest), Danger (61 more), and a Weekly pack that changes each week with its own leaderboard reset. Custom packs mix pets from packs the player owns. ([Wikipedia, Super Auto Pets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Auto_Pets))

**The Bazaar**
- Pick a hero, then over each day meet merchants, events, a PvE monster and an async PvP opponent; heroes have their own items and later expansion packs. ([Game8](https://game8.co/articles/games/the-bazaar); [Mobalytics review](https://mobalytics.gg/news/guides/the-bazaar-review)) [snippet] The project began as a deck builder and was rebuilt as a hero builder. ([The Bazaar wiki](https://thebazaar.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Bazaar))

**Backpack Battles**
- Classes went from 2 in the June 2023 demo to 4 at early access and 6 at 1.0 (Adventurer and Mage added 13 June 2025). ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles)) A store style listing says each class has 5 subclasses. [snippet] ([store listing mirror](https://www.co-op.gg/dos/game/backpack-battles))
- IGN's early access review (15 Mar 2024, 6/10) praised the shop phase but said there were not enough items to keep many runs interesting. ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles))

**Others**
- Storybook Brawl drew both reactions: Screen Rant (July 2021) said two rows and more varied mechanics boosted replay, while PC Gamer (June 2021) said too many random elements made games swing wildly. ([Wikipedia, Storybook Brawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storybook_Brawl))
- Underlords added City Crawl content and a battle pass with Season 1 at full release (Feb 2020) [snippet] ([3DJuegos](https://www.3djuegos.com/juegos/dota-underlords/noticias/dota-underlords-detalla-todas-las-caracteristicas-de-su-200221-101889); [GameStar](https://www.gamestar.de/artikel/dota-underlords-update-release-season-1-enno,3353854.html)), then a rotation of 8 new heroes and a Classic mode in Aug 2020 ([GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2020/08/dota-underlords-gets-a-big-reset-new-heroes-and-a-mode-without-underlords)).
- Clash Royale's Merge Tactics mode runs seasons; one guide says that since 1 Sep 2026 four troops are drawn at random per match. [snippet] ([PlayNews](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/merge-tactics-clash-royale-the-cavalry-trait-and-5-elixir-supers-4-troops-drawn-at-random-per-match-since-september-1st))

### Inferences
- Variety works at three scales in these games: each fight (the shop), each run (hero, augment, portal, anomaly, pack, class) and each season (new set, pack or heroes). A single player game with no live sets has to carry the run and season layers itself.
- Agency and variance pull against each other (Riot's Armory versus Chosen). The pick one of three offer is the compromise that lasted in both TFT and Battlegrounds.
- Novelty over nostalgia (Riot) and IGN's item pool complaint suggest the amount of content matters for retention, not only the systems.
- For Kindred Siege (implication, not a design call): tribes that learn the player's habits could act as a rule changer that differs per run, but portals and anomalies are shown before the player commits, so any learned change would likely need to be visible early to feel fair. Inference from reveal timing in TFT and Battlegrounds.

### Gaps
- How many minion types are active per Battlegrounds lobby was not confirmed.
- Backpack Battles subclass unlock timing was not confirmed; one early guide said after five wins in a row.
- No public usage data shows which variety layer keeps players longest.

## 4. Session length and the one more game loop

### Takeaway
Typical lengths: TFT about 25 to 35 minutes, Battlegrounds about 20 to 25 by player estimate, Backpack Battles fights of 10 to 20 seconds inside a run of up to 18 rounds, and async designs with no timers (Super Auto Pets Arena, The Bazaar). Riot's 20 minute TFT mode failed on engagement, and Riot now says it does not push players to log in daily.

### Cited Findings
- TFT: one guide puts a typical game at 25 to 35 minutes, with over 40 rare [community] ([1v9](https://1v9.gg/blog/how-long-is-a-tft-game)); British Esports lists 20 to 35 ([British Esports](https://britishesports.org/the-hub/esports-resources/games/team-fight-tactics/)). No official Riot average was found.
- Riot's first set review: at launch games ended too fast, then only the late game mattered; pacing is shaped by resources and player damage. ([Riot, Set 1 learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-tft-set-1-learnings/))
- Hyper Roll was pitched as finishing in 20 minutes or sooner [snippet] ([Riot, Hyper Roll](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-hyper-roll/)). It averaged about 20 minutes ([Riot, Reckoning learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-reckoning-learnings/)). Players who liked it valued lower stress and a smaller time commitment ([Riot, Gizmos & Gadgets learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-gizmos-gadgets-learnings/)). It was retired in 2025 at around 2% of playtime [snippet] ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/teamfight-tactics/tft-roadmap-2025-no-more-hyper-roll-pengus-party-returns-and-new-set-revival)).
- A GDC 2020 talk by TFT's lead designer said the team kept auto chess's relaxed pace and non confrontational play, and limited bench juggling because it added stress. [snippet] ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/teamfight-tactics-design); [GDC](https://gdconf.com/news/get-key-game-design-insights-riots-teamfight-tactics-gdc-2020))
- In 2026 Alex Cole said Riot avoids pushing players to log in every day and wants TFT to be a favorite second game that players drop in and out of by set. [snippet] ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/teamfight-tactics-turns-7-riot-games-discusses-lessons-learned-and-the-road-ahead-exclusive-interview/))
- Battlegrounds: player estimates put the average around 20 to 25 minutes, and 10 to 15 if you finish bottom four [community] ([Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/how-long-does-a-battlegrounds-match-take/17754)). The recruit timer is 60 seconds in round 1, plus 15 a round, capped at 120 ([Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds)).
- Super Auto Pets: Arena is async with no timers, while Versus is a live 8 player game with quick decisions ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1597449908)). The developers said they wanted a competitive auto battler where players have time to think ([itch.io](https://itch.io/t/1355958/super-auto-pets-chill-online-auto-battler)). [snippet]
- Backpack Battles: up to 18 rounds; fights usually last 10 to 20 seconds; win 10 before losing 5, then optional survival to round 18. ([Wikipedia, Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles))
- The Bazaar: each day runs six hours (two shop or event hours, a PvE fight, two more shop or event hours, a PvP fight), and a run ends at 10 wins or when prestige runs out [snippet] ([Mobalytics prestige guide](https://mobalytics.gg/the-bazaar/guides/prestige)). Opponents are recordings of players from up to several hours earlier, which one forum poster says avoids queues and quitters [community] [snippet] ([RPGHQ forum](https://rpghq.org/forums/viewtopic.php?p=169701)).
- Underlords was criticised in 2019 as feeling like a mobile port and too long for mobile. [opinion] ([Inven Global, 29 Dec 2019](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10060/2019-started-as-the-year-of-the-autobattler-but-did-it-end-that-way-too))
- Tocker's Trials has 30 rounds and no time limit. ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18925/when-does-pve-mode-tockers-trials-come-to-teamfight-tactics))
- Offered one more game, 69% of students picked the one they had narrowly won. [peer reviewed] ([BPS Research Digest](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/why-risk-losing-more-fun-easy-win))

### Inferences
- Shorter is not automatically stickier. Riot's 20 minute mode lost because it removed slack and stayed hard to learn, not because players wanted long games.
- The biggest games sit at 20 to 40 minutes per game. Nobody publishes retention by game length, so that is a correlation at best.
- Async ghosts let a run pause between fights; live lobbies cannot. A single player run has the same freedom, so the one more game pull has to come from the run's ending (close result, a reward, an unseen option) rather than from a lobby.

### Gaps
- No official average length for TFT, Battlegrounds, Super Auto Pets or The Bazaar; The Bazaar run length in minutes was not found.
- Valve's shorter Underlords modes were not checked before the search budget ran out.

## 5. Meta progression: unlocks, permanent upgrades, grind

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and mostly designer and player opinion: unlocks that add options (Slay the Spire style, Hades weapons) are praised, flat power or currency grinds draw criticism, and Hearthstone Mercenaries, a grind heavy PvE party battler, lost content support after about 16 months. Paid power or paid choice in PvP auto battlers caused backlash (The Bazaar open beta), and no peer reviewed study on meta progression and retention was found.

### Cited Findings
- Supergiant's Greg Kasavin said reducing the sting of failure was a big focus of Hades from the start, and that the brutal difficulty of roguelikes works against their replayability. The Mirror of Night lets players spend run currency on permanent upgrades and can be reset. [snippet] ([Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-mirror-of-night-upgrades-fated-list-of-prophecies)) God Mode starts at 20% damage reduction and adds 2% per death up to 80%; Kasavin chose it over a normal easy mode because dying advances the story. [snippet] ([Can I Play That](https://caniplaythat.com/2021/08/11/hades-god-mode-explained-by-supergiant-games))
- Hearthstone Mercenaries launched Oct 2021. Players cited an excess coin economy and no end game, and the economy took about a year to address. Blizzard's final big update came with patch 25.4 (Feb 2023), after which the mode got only maintenance and occasional balance changes while Blizzard moved focus to the main game and Battlegrounds. [snippet] ([BlizzardWatch, 7 Feb 2023](https://blizzardwatch.com/2023/02/07/hearthstone-mercenaries-mode-ends/); [Outof.games](https://outof.games/news/5983-hearthstone-mercenaries-support-ending-no-more-regular-content-updates-after-254/?page=2)) One outlet summed it up as poor monetization, low replayability with a dull grind and unbalanced PvP. [opinion] ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/hearthstone/hearthstone-mercenaries-will-no-longer-receive-content-updates/)) Weeks after launch players replayed one bounty over and over to reach a character that sped up levelling, until Blizzard patched it. [snippet] ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/15461/hearthstone-mercenaries-fans-up-in-arms-over-secret-hotfix-to-mysterious-stranger))
- Loop Hero, a single player roguelite with automatic fights and a persistent camp, sold 500,000 copies in its first week (Mar 2021) and a million by Dec 2021. [snippet] ([GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2021/12/loop-hero-from-four-quarters-hits-a-million-sales))
- Forum discussion separates sidegrades from flat power. Hades weapons are cited as mostly sidegrades. Some players say power upgrades made runs too easy and turned the game into grinding for the next unlock; others like the game getting easier without having to get better. A Tiny Rogues developer described the two poles as having to invest before you can finish versus unlocking content only. [community] [snippet] ([Feddit thread](https://feddit.it/post/5081108); [Tiny Rogues Steam post](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2088570/discussions/2/3361397532265670884))
- Slay the Spire is often cited as unlock only, with no power from meta progression, acting like a gradual tutorial; some argue its Ascension levels are a form of meta progression. [community] [snippet] ([hamatti notes](https://notes.hamatti.org/Gaming/Video-games/Meta-progression-with-gradual-tutorial-in-roguelike-games); [Lemmy comment](https://lemm.ee/comment/11446768))
- The Bazaar open beta (Mar 2025): free daily ranked tickets were replaced by a 100 gem price (about $1) and some hero expansion cards were locked behind a paid battle pass. Players called it pay to win, and Reynad answered combatively on Reddit. ([Cardgamer](https://cardgamer.com/games/digital-card-games/the-bazaar/6-reasons-why-the-bazaars-open-beta-caused-an-uproar) [snippet]; [PC Gamer, syndicated on Yahoo, 9 Apr 2025](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/back-month-spent-strangling-itself-161941715.html)) About a month later Tempo removed the paid tier, made expansions earnable (and free at season end for players short of XP), flattened levelling and restored a free daily ranked ticket, and the subreddit turned positive. ([PC Gamer, syndicated on Yahoo](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/back-month-spent-strangling-itself-161941715.html))
- Battlegrounds sells extra hero choice: the paid Season Pass offers 4 heroes instead of 2. ([Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds))
- Clash Mini, Supercell's standalone auto battler, ended development in March 2024; one analyst attributes this to weak retention and monetization [opinion]. The concept returned in 2025 as Merge Tactics inside Clash Royale. [snippet] ([Eric Guan](https://ericguan.substack.com/p/2games1app); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/supercell-releases-major-clash-royale-update-with-new-game-mode-merge-tactics))

### Inferences
- What players punish is grind that gates power, and money that buys power or choice. What they accept is unlocks that widen options or soften failure without deciding the outcome.
- Riot's stance against daily login pressure (section 4) is the opposite of the play by appointment pattern in the dark patterns literature (section 7).
- Kindred Siege's tribes that learn across runs is itself a meta layer, but one that pushes against the player instead of for them. No evidence either way on that pattern was found.

### Gaps
- No peer reviewed study on meta progression and retention or churn was found.
- A Game Rant review of Loop Hero that mentions grinding for camp materials appeared in a search summary, but its URL was not captured, so it is not cited.
- No official Mega Crit statement on Slay the Spire's unlock design was checked; adaptive difficulty research (relevant to learning tribes) was not searched.

The genre went from one mod (Jan 2019) to several big games within months, then most standalone entries shrank fast. The survivors live inside a huge parent game (TFT, Battlegrounds, Merge Tactics) or are cheap indie async games that sell hundreds of thousands of copies and settle at a few thousand Steam players; Underlords fell below 1% of its peak after Valve's updates stopped in 2020, and Storybook Brawl closed after its owner FTX collapsed.

## 7. Research on engagement: reinforcement, flow, mastery, self determination theory, ethics

### Takeaway
Peer reviewed work supports three relevant claims: games pull players by meeting needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness; close contests raise enjoyment through suspense; and near misses raise the urge to continue when the player had control. The variable ratio reinforcement explanation is an analogy carried over from gambling and loot box research and is untested on auto battler shops; play time alone is not linked to lower wellbeing, and the clearest harm evidence sits where randomness meets money.

### Cited Findings
**Peer reviewed**
- Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski (Motivation and Emotion, 2006, 30(4)): across four studies, perceived autonomy and competence in play were tied to enjoyment, game preference and short term wellbeing changes. In an online multiplayer community, autonomy, competence and relatedness each independently predicted enjoyment and future play. [snippet] ([ACU Research Bank](https://acuresearchbank.acu.edu.au/item/8q128/the-motivational-pull-of-video-games-a-self-determination-theory-approach); [University of Rochester release](https://www.rochester.edu/news/printable.php?id=2719)) The work led to the Player Experience of Need Satisfaction measure. [snippet] ([Center for Self Determination Theory](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/player-experience-of-needs-satisfaction-pens/))
- Abuhamdeh, Csikszentmihalyi and Jalal (Motivation and Emotion, 2015, 39(1)): suspense and competence are separate sources of enjoyment; the authors argue the competence motive can be outweighed by enjoyment of suspense, and link this to flow occurring at the limits of ability. ([BPS Research Digest](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/why-risk-losing-more-fun-easy-win)) An earlier Abuhamdeh and Csikszentmihalyi study tied enjoyment to challenge ([USUHS listing](https://scholar.usuhs.edu/en/publications/the-importance-of-challenge-for-the-enjoyment-of-intrinsically-mo/)); a blog summary puts the sweet spot near an opponent about 7% stronger, not checked against the paper ([A Learning a Day](https://alearningaday.blog/2014/08/10/7-better-the-200-words-project/)). [snippet]
- Clark and colleagues (Neuron, 2009): near misses and personal control, as in section 1. ([PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2658737/))
- Vuorre, Johannes, Magnusson and Przybylski (Royal Society Open Science, 27 July 2022): about 39,000 adults, play logs donated by seven publishers, six weeks. Little to no evidence that play time causally affects wellbeing; motivations do play a role. ([Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/major-new-study-finds-little-evidence-for-causal-connection-between-well-being-and-video-game-playing/); [PsyPost](https://www.psypost.org/playing-video-games-appears-to-have-no-significant-influence-on-well-being-according-to-a-large-study))
- Stevens, Dorstyn, Delfabbro and King (Australian and New Zealand Journal of Psychiatry, 2021, 55(6)): 53 studies, 226,247 people, 17 countries. Pooled gaming disorder prevalence 3.05% (95% CI 2.38 to 3.91), or 1.96% in studies with stricter sampling. The screening tool explained 77% of the variance, and rates were higher in adolescents and in males (about 2.5 to 1). [snippet] ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33028074/))
- Spicer and colleagues (New Media & Society, 2022): loot box buying and problem gambling r = 0.27, with 12 of 13 publications positive; problem gaming r = 0.40 from only six surveys; direction of cause unclear; funded by GambleAware. ### Inferences
- Mapped to self determination theory: pick one of three offers and opted in risks feed autonomy; readable outcomes and adapting to variance feed competence; lobbies and ghosts feed relatedness. A single player game has no PvP channel for relatedness, so it would need another source (story, characters, or other players' recorded runs). Untested inference.
- Kindred Siege's fight orders are a form of personal control. The near miss study found control was the condition that made near misses motivating, so orders could make close losses pull harder. That is the same lever that worries gambling researchers, so the honest version is real close fights, not manufactured ones. Inference.
- The harm evidence is strongest where randomness meets money (loot boxes) and where play is forced by schedules (play by appointment). In a premium single player game with free shop rolls those links are unstudied; the more likely risk is grind, a temporal dark pattern.

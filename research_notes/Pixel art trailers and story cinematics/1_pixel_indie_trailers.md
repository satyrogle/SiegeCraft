# Pixel and 2D indie trailers, shot by shot (researcher 1, 16 Oct 2026)

Method: 8 official trailers sampled frame by frame, muted (5 to 20 frames each). Voice, music and beat sync are not checked for any of them. Lengths, dates and views read from YouTube page data on 16 Oct 2026. Gameplay share figures are the researcher's estimates (opinion). No source tied wishlists or sales to a specific trailer.

## Bottom line
- The trailers analysts praise put play on screen inside about 4 seconds, or one card then play. They land the hook by about 15 s.
- Laughs come from deadpan text cards and deaths, not from story.
- The three that open on story (Cult of the Lamb, Kingdom Rush Vengeance, Hades) used full hand animation and a big publisher, franchise or showcase slot.
- None of the trailers checked used story stills.

## What analysts say about openings
- Slow burn reveals suit live showcase audiences, not Steam shoppers; gameplay first appearing 15 to 30 s in is too late (Simon Carless's estimate, not measured). Derek Lieu now opens with a few seconds of play so the game is moving before viewers scrub; Chris Zukowski found viewers skip through the bar looking for raw gameplay. [GameDiscoverCo, 19 Sep 2022](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/game-trailers-breaking-down-a-key)
- Steam shoppers are most impatient with slow panning cinematic shots, logos and footage with no player interaction. Lieu's minimum Steam trailer: 90 to 180 s, 3 to 5 s shots, nothing shown more than three times, HUD on, core loop first, no logos or non interactive cinematics. [Game Developer, 19 Apr 2021](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/the-simplest-trailer-to-make-for-your-steam-page)
- Logos: no opening logos unless people know the studio; 1 s each at most; No More Robots' retention graphs showed no difference; preferred "sandwich" of play, quick logos, play. [Lieu, Mar 2021](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2021/3/29/where-to-put-logos-in-a-game-trailer)
- Hades II early access: feature cards go full screen then sit at the top of frame so it reads muted; its long intro without play only works for an established studio. [Lieu, May 2024](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2024/5/19/hades-ii-early-access-trailer-review)
- Outline: optional cold open, introduction, escalation, climax, optional button. Order is genre, then hook, then content; long "anchors" make a game look like a lesser copy. [Lieu, Feb 2021](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2021/2/28/game-trailer-outline-based-on-genre); [GameDiscoverCo, 7 Nov 2022](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-to-get-to-your-games-hook-quick)

## How jokes and premise land
- Vampire Survivors: card boast then undercut (120,000 positive reviews, then 0 vampires); Lieu's Best Copy of 2022. [Lieu 2022](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2022/12/23/my-favorite-game-trailers-of-2022)
- Shadows Over Loathing: pedigree line, then a typography gag turns the genre label into comic horror (honourable mention for copy, same post).
- Noita 1.0 (Lieu's cut): disasters ending in death as punchlines; deliberate pause at a lantern smash before a chain reaction; captured in 4K with debug tools (invincibility, spawning, camera lock), nearly 4 hours of footage over a week. [Lieu, Oct 2020](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2020/10/17/noita-10-launch-trailer)
- Dead Cells launch shows the resurrection animation three times (rule of threes: show a hook two or three times). [Lieu, Nov 2022](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2022/11/14/the-rule-of-threes-in-game-trailers)
- Thimbleweed Park: cold open, town, characters, one big claim each, end montage; Lieu sorted over 10,000 lines. [Lieu, Mar 2017](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2017/3/30/thimbleweed-park-launch-trailer)
- Hooks can backfire if the trailer concept overshadows the game (Chronos). [Lieu, Aug 2021](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2021/8/30/trailers-with-their-own-hook)
- The one scary thing: Inscryption's eyes in the dark; Kingdom Rush Vengeance's glowing silhouette at 25 s; Cult of the Lamb's red eyes behind the altar at 10 s.

## Music and cut timing
- Pick the track first; cut cards and big hits on hard beats; game sounds as percussion, nudged a few frames to sync (Lieu's Dead Cells trailers); heavy sync can wrongly suggest a rhythm mechanic. [Lieu, Feb 2021](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2021/2/1/sound-design-driven-game-trailers)
- Hades II music map: 0:00 to 0:07 sound design lead in, cue on strong beats from 0:08, layers added, second cue 1:18, reprise 2:03, series riff over the title 2:38. It is usually fatal when skipping around inside a cue changes nothing. [Lieu, May 2024](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2024/5/19/hades-ii-early-access-trailer-review)
- No sound effects is a fault (Lieu on a 2022 trailer). Unpacking praised for a chiptune score with a simple arc, faulted for no call to action.

## Praised
Vampire Survivors (copy), Dome Keeper (2D cinematic gameplay, which Lieu notes is much harder with pixel art), Cult of the Lamb launch and Hades II reveal (paid animation studios), Dead Cells hand drawn trailer (novelty), Don't Scream (hook in title and opening text), Manifold Garden (hook and anchor in one shot), Spelunky 2 (developer commentary that looks like raw play when muted). Sources: Lieu's 2022 and 2023 favourites posts, GameDiscoverCo Nov 2022.

## Researcher's copy list for Kindred Siege (all opinion)
1. 0 to 4 s: in engine play, or one deadpan card then play; never a stills montage.
2. Joke inside 5 s: a two card setup and undercut; the premise's own pair is the cult's boast, then the wipe.
3. The Sovereign as an effect, never a body: eyes or a silhouette in the dark, plus half the army gone, with a stop beat before it.
4. Genre by about 10 s: side view 2.5D fights with the HUD on.
5. Hook by 15 to 30 s, shown two or three times: an order changing a fight, tribes adapting.
6. Deaths and nerve breaks as punchlines, about three times.
7. Cards at the top of frame, readable muted, written with a voice.
8. Track first; fight sounds as percussion.
9. End on a button: one last joke or one last trace of her, then logo and call to action.
10. Capture with debug camera and tools; integer scale.
